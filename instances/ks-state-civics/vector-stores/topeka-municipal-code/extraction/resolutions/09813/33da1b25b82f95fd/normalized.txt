3 A RESOLUTION introduced by Public Infrastructure Committee recommending

4 approval of Project Nos. 841099.11, 501125.02 and 281300.12 for

street and utility improvements on SE 37th Street from Adams Street

to east of Indiana Avenue

WHEREAS, the Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 9723 approving the

2026-2035 Capital Improvement Program (CIP) and the 2026-2028 Capital Improvement

Budget (CIB); and

WHEREAS, the Resolution requires Governing Body approval for projects that are

ready for construction and whose total project budget exceeds $250,000; and

WHEREAS, on May 19, 2026, the Public Infrastructure Committee recommended

approval of the project.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that Project Nos. 841099.11, 501125.02 and 281300.12

for street and utility improvements on SE 37th Street from Adams Street to east of Indiana

Avenue as further described in Exhibit A is hereby approved.

ADOPTED and APPROVED by the Governing Body on June 2, 2026.
