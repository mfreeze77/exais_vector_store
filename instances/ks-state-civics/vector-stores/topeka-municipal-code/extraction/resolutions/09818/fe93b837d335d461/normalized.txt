RESOLUTION NO. 9818

A RESOLUTION introduced by Councilmember Karen Hiller granting Randy Wheat

an exception to the provisions of City of Topeka Code Section

9.45.150, et seq. concerning noise prohibitions. 6

WHEREAS, City of Topeka Code Section 9.45.150, et seq. makes it

unlawful for any person to make, continue or cause to be made or continued any

loud, unnecessary or unusual noise or any noise which either annoys, disturbs,

injures or endangers the comfort, repose, health or safety or others within the

limits of the city; and

WHEREAS, City of Topeka Code Section 9.45.170, et seq. authorizes the

City Council to grant exceptions to the prohibitions of this code section upon

request and a showing that the proposed activity does not offend the spirit of the

findings of City of Topeka Code Section 9.45.150, et seq.; and

WHEREAS, Randy Wheat has requested that they be granted an

exception to the provisions of City of Topeka Code Section 9.45.150, et seq. for

the purposes, dates and times described herein, and

WHEREAS, upon review of the application of Randy Wheat the

Governing Body of the City of Topeka does hereby find that the requested

activity does not offend the spirit of the findings of City of Topeka Code Section 22 9.45.150, et seq.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of the

City of Topeka Randy Wheat is hereby granted an exception from the provisions

of City of Topeka Code Section 9.45.150, et seq. for the Midwest Demo Kings

Juneteenth located at 301 SW 10th Avenue, during the hours of 11:00 a.m. and

27 4:00 p.m. on June 19, 2026.
