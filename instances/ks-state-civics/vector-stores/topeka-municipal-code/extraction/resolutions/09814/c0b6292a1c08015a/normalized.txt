RESOLUTION NO. 9814

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez amending the 2026-2035 CIP and the 2026-2028 CIB to add Project No. 121052.00 for bridge deck repairs at three locations throughout the City.

WHEREAS, the Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 9723 approving the 2026-

2035 Capital Improvement Program (CIP) and the 2026-2028 Capital Improvement Budget

(CIB); and

WHEREAS, the Resolution requires Governing Body approval for projects that are

ready for construction and whose total project budget exceeds $250,000; and

WHEREAS, the City Manager recommends amending the CIP/CIB to include a

project for bridge deck repairs and recommends final approval of the project.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that

(1) The 2026-2035 CIP and 2026-2028 CIB be amended to include Project No.

121052.00 for bridge deck repairs at three locations throughout the City as further

described in the Capitol Improvement Project Summary (Exhibit A) which is attached

herein and incorporated by reference.

(2) Project No. 121052.00 as further described in Exhibit B is hereby approved.

ADOPTED and APPROVED by the Governing Body on June 2, 2026.
