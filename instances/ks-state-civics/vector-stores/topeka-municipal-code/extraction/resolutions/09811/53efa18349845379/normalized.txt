A RESOLUTION introduced by Public Infrastructure Committee recommending

approval of Project No. 701070.00 for street improvements to SW

45th Street, from Gage Boulevard to Cambridge Avenue.

WHEREAS, the Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 9723 approving the2026-

2035 Capital Improvement Program (CIP) and the 2026-2028 Capital Improvement Budget

(CIB); and

WHEREAS, the Resolution requires Governing Body approval for projects that are

ready for construction and whose total project budget exceeds $250,000; and

WHEREAS, on May 19, 2026, the Public Infrastructure Committee recommended

approval of the project(s).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that Project No. 701070.00 for street improvements to SW

45th Street from Gage Boulevard to Cambridge Avenue as further described in Exhibit A

is hereby approved.

ADOPTED and APPROVED by the Governing Body on June 2, 2026.
