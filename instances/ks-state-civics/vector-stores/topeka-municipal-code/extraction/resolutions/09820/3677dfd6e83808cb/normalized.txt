RESOLUTION NO. 9820

A RESOLUTION introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez, authorizing the City Attorney’s Office to initiate litigation.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that the City Attorney’s Office is hereby authorized to initiate a lawsuit, seeking a judgment for damages resulting from a breach of contract created by Ordinance 20252, in the Shawnee County District Court.

ADOPTED and APPROVED by the Governing Body on June 16, 2026.

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS

Spencer L. Duncan, Mayor

ATTEST:

Brenda Younger, City Clerk
