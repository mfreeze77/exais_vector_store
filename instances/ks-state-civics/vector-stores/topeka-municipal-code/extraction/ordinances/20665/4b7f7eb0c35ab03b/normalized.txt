(Published in the Topeka Metro News on June 29, 2026)

ORDINANCE NO. 20665

AN ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez, removing

certain real property from the Capital City Townhomes TIF

Redevelopment District.

WHEREAS, Resolution No. 9796 approves the Capital City Townhomes project as

an eligible project under the Neighborhood Revitalization Program (“NRP”); and

WHEREAS, the project property located is located in a Tax Increment Financing

district created by Ordinance No. 20651; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 4f. of Resolution No. 9796 the property must be

removed from the TIF district; and

WHEREAS, K.S.A. 12-1771(g) authorizes the Governing Body to remove real

property from a redevelopment district; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that:

Section 1. The following properties are removed from the District:

• 2020 SE Massachusetts Avenue

• 2021 SE Massachusetts Avenue

• 2023 SE Pennsylvania Street

• 2024 SE Massachusetts Avenue

• 2025 SE Massachusetts Avenue

• 2027 SE Pennsylvania Street

• 2029 SE Massachusetts Avenue

• 2033 SE Massachusetts Avenue

Section 2. The City Clerk is directed to provide a copy of the Ordinance to the

County Clerk.

Section 3. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its

passage, approval and publication in the official city newspaper.

PASSED and APPROVED by the Governing Body June 16, 2026.
