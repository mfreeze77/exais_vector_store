ORDINANCE NO. 20660

AN ORDINANCE INTRODUCED BY CITY MANAGER DR. ROBERT M.

PEREZ ESTABLISHING A REINVESTMENT HOUSING

INCENTIVE DISTRICT (“RHID”) FOR THE WINDWARD

ESTATES DISTRICT; AND ADOPTING A PLAN FOR THE

DEVELOPMENT OF HOUSING AND PUBLIC FACILITIES

IN THE RHID.

WHEREAS, on October 30, 2025, the Kansas Secretary of Commerce determined that the

findings by the Governing Body articulated in Resolution No. 9721 meet the requirements of

K.S.A. 12-5244 and that the Governing Body may proceed with establishing the RHID; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to K.S.A. 12-5245(b), the Governing Body of the City of Topeka

(the “City”) has heretofore adopted Resolution No. 9795, which stated that the City is considering

establishing the proposed RHID and adopting a proposed development plan for the RHID, set forth

the boundaries of the proposed RHID, provided a summary of the proposed development plan,

called a public hearing concerning the establishment of the proposed RHID, and provided for

notice of such public hearing, as provided in K.S.A. 12-5245; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body, on June 2, 2026, held a public hearing to consider

public comment concerning the establishment of the RHID and adoption of a plan for the

development of housing and public facilities; and

WHEREAS, upon considering the information provided by the developer (Windward

Estates LLC, a Kansas limited liability company), City staff and comments from the public, the

Governing Body deems it advisable to make certain findings to establish the proposed RHID and

to adopt the proposed plan.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that:

Section 1. Establishment of the Windward Estates RHID. The Windward Estates RHID

will be established within the boundaries of the real estate legally described and attached in

Exhibits A and B. The boundaries of the RHID do not contain any property not referenced in

Resolution No. 9795, which provided notice of the public hearing.

Section 2. Approval of Development Plan (“Plan”). The Plan for the development of

housing and public facilities presented on April 21, 2026 to the Governing Body, which includes

(a) the “Application for Creation of a RHID Housing Development Plan” received by the City on

January 21, 2026, and (b) a “City of Topeka, Kansas Windward Estates LLC RHID Project Plan

Financial Analysis,” dated March 20, 2026, contains the requirements specified in K.S.A. 12-

5245(a) and is hereby approved.

Section 3. Adverse Effect on Other Governmental Units. If, within 30 days following

the conclusion of the public hearing, any of the following actions occur, this ordinance shall be

null and void.

(a) The Board of Education for U.S.D. 450 determines, by resolution, the proposed

RHID will have an adverse effect on the school district.

(b) The Board of Commissioners for Shawnee County determines, by resolution, the

proposed RHID will have an adverse effect on the county.

Section 4. Transmission of Documents. The City Clerk is directed to transmit a copy of

the description of the land within the RHID, a copy of this ordinance adopting the Plan and a map

or plat indicating the boundaries of the RHID to: (a) the Shawnee County Clerk; (b) the Shawnee

County Appraiser; (c) Shawnee County Treasurer; and (d) the governing bodies of any taxing

subdivision which levies taxes upon any property in the RHID.

Section 5. Effective Date. This ordinance shall take effect after its passage and approval

by the Governing Body and publication in the official City newspaper.

PASSED by the Governing Body on June 2, 2026, and APPROVED AND

SIGNED by the Mayor.

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS
