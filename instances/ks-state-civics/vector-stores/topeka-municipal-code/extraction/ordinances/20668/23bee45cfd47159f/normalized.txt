ORDINANCE NO. 20668

AN ORDINANCE INTRODUCED BY CITY MANAGER DR. ROBERT M. PEREZ

TERMINATING THE CAPITAL CITY TOWNHOMES REDEVELOPMENT

DISTRICT CREATED PURSUANT TO ORDINANCE NO. 20651.

WHEREAS, the City of Topeka Kansas (the “City”), is a city of the first-class organized and

existing under the constitution and laws of the State of Kansas; and

WHEREAS, the City created a Redevelopment District pursuant to K.S.A. 12-1770 et seq., as

amended, and Ordinance No. 20651 for the real property legally described on Exhibit A attached hereto

(the “Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District”); and

WHEREAS, the City, by Ordinance No. 20526, has previously established a Neighborhood

Revitalization program (the “NRP”) and approved a Neighborhood Revitalization Plan (the “NRP Plan”)

pursuant to K.S.A. 12-17,114 et seq., as amended, for the purpose of promoting the revitalization and

development of designated areas within the City; and

WHEREAS, a project consisting of rehabilitation and improvement of a 47-unit living community

of eleven buildings within the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District (the “Project”) has been

approved, by Resolution No. 9796 approved by the Governing Body of the City, for the “Standard Plus 20

Rebate” set forth in the NRP Plan; and

WHEREAS, the NRP Plan provides that because the Project has been approved for the “Stand

Plus 20 Rebate” the Project property should be removed from the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment

District; and

WHEREAS, because the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District is comprised entirely

of the Project property, and the Governing Body has passed Ordinance No. 20665 to remove the Project

property from the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District, the Governing Body has determined

601528.20189\TIF TERMINATION ORDINANCE (CAPITAL CITY TOWNHOMES)

that it is necessary and desirable to pass this Ordinance to terminate the Capital City Townhomes

Redevelopment District.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF

TOPEKA, KANSAS, AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Termination of Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District. The City

hereby terminates the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District and terminates any tax increment

financing authority for the Capital City Townhomes Redevelopment District.

Section 2. Further Authority. The City shall, and the officers, employees and agents of the

City are hereby authorized and directed to, take such action, expend such funds and execute such other

documents, certificates and instruments as may be necessary or desirable to carry out and comply with the

intent of this Ordinance. The City Clerk is authorized and directed to transmit a copy of this Ordinance to

the governing body, clerk, assessor and treasurer of Shawnee County, and to the governing body of Unified

School District No. 501, Shawnee County, Kansas (Topeka).

Section 3. Governing Law. This Ordinance shall be governed by and construed in

accordance with the applicable laws of the State of Kansas.

Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in full force from and after

its passage by the Governing Body of the City and publication in the official City newspaper.

PASSED by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka on July 14, 2026, AND APPROVED AND SIGNED by the Mayor.
