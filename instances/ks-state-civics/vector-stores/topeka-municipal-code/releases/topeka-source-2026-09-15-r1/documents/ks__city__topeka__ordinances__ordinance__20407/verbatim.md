## (Published in the Topeka Metro News February 13, 2023)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20407

- AN ORDINANCE introduced  by  City  Manager  Stephen  Wade  adopting  the   2021 International Fire  Code,  amending  Chapter 14.40  of  the  Topeka Municipal Code and repealing original section.
- BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That section 14.40.010, Adoption of 2015 International Fire   Code., of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Adoption of 20152021 International Fire Code.

There is hereby adopted by reference, and incorporated herein as if fully set   out in   this  chapter,   the   20152021  International  Fire   Code,  as  amended,      including Appendices B, C, D, E, and F, G, H, I and N, except as hereafter provided in Article II of this chapter.

Section 2. That section 14.40.020, Copies on file in Clerk's office, of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Copies on file in Clerk's office.

At least one  copy of  the  20152021  International Fire Code as adopted  in    this chapter shall be on file with the City Clerk to be available for inspection by the public   at all  reasonable  business   hours.  The  Police   Department,   Municipal  Court  and    all administrative  departments  of  the  City  charged  with  the  duty of  enforcement of this chapter shall be supplied, at the cost of the City, such number of copies of the code   as may be deemed expedient by the Fire Chief.

## Chapter 1, Section 105.6.325.34, Open burning.

Chapter 1, Section 105.6.325.34, Open burning, is hereby deleted in its    entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:

An operational permit is required for the kindling or maintaining of an open fire  or a fire on any public street, alley, road, or other public or private ground. Instructions  and stipulations of the permit shall be adhered to.

Exception: Recreational Fires defined at subsections (a) and (b):

Section 7. That section 14.40.070, Chapter 3, Section 304.1.2, Vegetation,   of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Chapter 31, Section 304.1.2, Vegetation109.3, Recordkeeping.

Chapter 31, Section 304.1.2, Vegetation109.3, Recordkeeping, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:

Weeds,  grass,  vines  or  other  growth  that  is  capable  of  being  ignited     and endangering property, shall be cut down and removed by the owner or occupant of   the premises. When the fire chief or their designee determines that total removal of   growth is  impractical  due  to  size  or  environmental  factors,  approved  fuel  breaks  shall  be established. Designated areas shall be cleared of combustible vegetation to establish fuel breaks. Vegetation clearance requirements in urban-wildland interface areas    shall be in accordance with the International Wildland-Urban Interface Code.

Records shall be made available for inspection by the fire code official,  and  a  copy  of  the  records  shall  be  provided  to  the  fire  code  official  on request.

Section 16. This ordinance shall take effect  on April 1, 2023, and be  in force from and after its passage, approval and publication in the official City newspaper.

Section 17. This ordinance shall supersede all ordinances, resolutions or  rules, or portions thereof, which are in conflict with the provisions of this ordinance.

Section 18. Should any section, clause or phrase of this ordinance be  declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the same shall not affect the validity of this ordinance as a whole, or any part thereof, other than the part so declared to be  invalid.
