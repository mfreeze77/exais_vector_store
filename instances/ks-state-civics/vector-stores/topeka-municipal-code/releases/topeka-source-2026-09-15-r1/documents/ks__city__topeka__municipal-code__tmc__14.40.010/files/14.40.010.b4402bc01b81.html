This article is included in your selections.
14.40.010 Adoption of 2021 International Fire Code.
This section is included in your selections.

There is hereby adopted by reference, and incorporated herein as if fully set out in this chapter, the 2021 International Fire Code, as amended, including Appendices B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I and N, except as hereafter provided in Article II of this chapter. (Ord. 20407 § 1, 2-7-23.)

State Law References:Adoption by reference authorized, K.S.A. 12-3009 et seq., 12-3301 et seq.
