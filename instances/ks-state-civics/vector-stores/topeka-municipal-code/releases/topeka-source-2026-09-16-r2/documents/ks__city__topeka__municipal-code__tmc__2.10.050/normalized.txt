All City offices keeping and maintaining open public records shall establish office hours during which any person may make a request for an open public record. Such hours shall be no fewer than the regular working hours for each day the office maintains regular office hours. (Ord. 20206 § 3, 9-10-19; Ord. 17226 § 16, 1-27-98. Code 1995 § 2-83. Formerly 2.20.050.)
