(a) Each applicant shall provide a certificate of liability insurance evidencing an insurance policy from an insurance company authorized to do business in the State of Kansas, which provides general liability coverage in an amount not less than $500,000 combined single limit per occurrence for bodily injury and property damage with the City named as an additional insured.
(b) Notwithstanding subsection (a) of this section, an applicant for a block party may execute an indemnity agreement in a form required by the City, indemnifying and holding harmless the City and its officers and employees against all claims, damages or causes of action arising from the event. (Ord. 20038 § 22, 12-6-16.)
