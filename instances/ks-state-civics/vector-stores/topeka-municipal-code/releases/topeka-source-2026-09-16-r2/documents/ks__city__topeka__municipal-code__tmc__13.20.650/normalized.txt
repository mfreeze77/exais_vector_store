If any public State or Federal agency imposes or threatens to impose any penalty upon the WPCD, the Utilities Director will immediately notify the person(s) or agency reasonably believed to be causing the WPCD to be in violation by virtue of their discharge to the City sewer. If a penalty is subsequently assessed against the WPCD, the person(s) or agency causing the WPCD to be in violation shall be held liable for the penalty. All fines imposed as a result of a violation of this chapter shall be paid by the person(s) or agency causing the violation to the City and credited to the WPCD. (Ord. 20157 § 8, 12-4-18.)
