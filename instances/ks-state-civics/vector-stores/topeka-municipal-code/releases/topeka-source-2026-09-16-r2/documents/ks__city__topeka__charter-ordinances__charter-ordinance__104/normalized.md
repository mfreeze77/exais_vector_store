## Reclaiming of operation by owners.

In  the  event  that  the  owners  of  the  land  within  a  cemetery  so  declared  to  be abandoned as provided in section 2 [A5-17] of this ordinance, shall desire to reclaim the operation  of  any  such  cemetery,  then  said  owners  may  file  with  the  board  of commissioners proof of their ownership in the form of a certificate of title certified by a licensed and bonded abstractor to be approved as to form by the city attorney together with an affidavit declaring the intent of said owner or owners to reclaim the operation of said abandoned cemetery and to pay the expenses which have been incurred by the City  of  Topeka  in  the  operation,  care  and  maintenance  thereof.  Upon  receipt  of  said certificate of title, affidavit and the payment  of such expenses, the board of commissioners shall, by resolution, declare said cemetery to be operational and shall cease to continue the operation, care and maintenance thereof.

Section 5. This  Ordinance  shall  be  published  once  each  week  for  two  (2) consecutive weeks in the official City newspaper.

Section 6. This  Charter  Ordinance  shall  take  effect  sixty-one  (61)  days  after final publication unless a sufficient petition for a referendum is filed and a referendum held on this Charter Ordinance as provided in Article 12, Section 5, Subdivision (c)(3), of the Kansas Constitution, in which case this Charter Ordinance shall become effective if approved by a majority of the electors voting therein.
