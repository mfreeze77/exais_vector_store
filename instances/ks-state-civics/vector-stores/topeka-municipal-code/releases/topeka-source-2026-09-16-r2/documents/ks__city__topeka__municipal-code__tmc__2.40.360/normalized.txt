Subject to the provisions of TMC 2.40.230 and amendments thereto, the Municipal Judge may compel the appearance of an accused person. In addition to the procedures provided in TMC 2.40.280 and amendments thereto, the Municipal Judge, upon request, may permit appearance, pleas and satisfaction of the judgment and sentence of the court by counsel or by mail. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 38, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-73. Formerly 2.110.360.)
