City staff will be available, upon reasonable notice to the City Manager, to assist the Committee in its work, and to provide such information and materials as may be requested by the Committee. The Committee may, at its pleasure, recruit the assistance of other citizens, scholars in the field, or professional municipal managers for participation on an ad hoc basis. (Ord. 20206 § 76, 9-10-19; Ord. 19320 § 8, 10-13-09. Code 1995 § 2-15. Formerly 2.13.060.)
