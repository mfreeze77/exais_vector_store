It shall be unlawful for pedestrians, persons operating or using bicycles or other nonmotorized traffic, persons leading, driving or riding animals or persons operating a motor-driven cycle of any type with a horsepower of less than five, to use the controlled access facilities and the interstate highway system within the corporate limits of the City. (Code 1981 § 43-165. Code 1995 § 142-183.)
State Law References: Authority to regulate or restrict use of controlled-access highways, K.S.A. 8-1525 , 8-2002(a)(13) .
