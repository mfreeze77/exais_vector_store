The Mayor will establish neighborhood improvement association (NIA) districts for the purpose of assigning rotating voting rights for NIA representatives to the Citizen Advisory Council. Each NIA district will consist of at least two NIAs. To the extent possible, NIA districts will consist of NIAs whose boundaries are contiguous. (Ord. 20206 § 8, 9-10-19; Ord. 18263 § 6, 6-8-04. Code 1981 § 13-11. Code 1995 § 42-6. Formerly 2.105.060.)
