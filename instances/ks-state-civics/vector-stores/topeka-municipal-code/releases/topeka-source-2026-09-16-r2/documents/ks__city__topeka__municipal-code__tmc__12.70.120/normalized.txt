(a) Other Permits, Licenses, Fees. The issuance of a special event permit does not relieve any person from the obligation to obtain any other permit or pay any additional fees required pursuant to this code or other state, City or County regulation.
(b) Limitations – Restrictions. The issuance of a special event permit does not relieve any person from restrictions or limitations contained in this code or State law relating to noise, consumption of cereal malt beverages or alcoholic liquor.
(c) Notwithstanding the issuance of a permit, the Police Chief or designee may cancel a special event in the interest of public safety. (Ord. 20038 § 26, 12-6-16.)
