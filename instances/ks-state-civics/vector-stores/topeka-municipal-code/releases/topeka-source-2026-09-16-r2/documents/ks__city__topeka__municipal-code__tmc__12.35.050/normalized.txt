The cost of construction, reconstruction or repair of sidewalks under TMC 12.35.060(c) , Condemnation and reconstruction, including the cost of preparing plans and specifications and providing supervision of such work, shall be levied as a service assessment under Chapter 3.45 TMC, Article III, on all the lots and pieces of land abutting on the sidewalk improvement according to the front foot thereof. (Ord. 17439 § 8, 12-7-99. Code 1981 § 40-168. Code 1995 § 130-376.)
