(a) The City Manager shall be responsible to the Governing Body for the proper and efficient administration of all affairs of the city under his or her jurisdiction, and shall, subject to the provisions of the personnel policies of this city and except as otherwise provided in this Charter Ordinance, have the power to appoint, assign, reassign, discipline, and remove all directors or heads of departments and all employees under the City Manager’s jurisdiction. The City Manager may delegate to directors or other department heads the authority to appoint, discipline, and remove subordinate employees, subject to the personnel policies of this city.
(b) Notwithstanding subsection (a) , in recognition that the City Attorney represents the municipal corporation rather than any one Governing Body member or employee and that conflicts may arise, prior to appointment, the City Manager shall give the Governing Body a meaningful opportunity to provide input on the City Manager’s proposed appointee for City Attorney. Additionally, the City Manager cannot remove the City Attorney unless approved by the Governing Body. In accordance with Charter Ordinances 94 §11 and 96 §3 as codified in Section A2-25 of the Topeka Municipal Code, the Governing Body shall have no unilateral removal power of the City Attorney. (C.O. 124 § 2, 6-11-24.)
