The provisions of this article shall apply to all departments, agencies, instrumentalities, funds or other organizations controlled directly or indirectly by the Council. The provisions of this article shall not apply to the Metropolitan Transit Authority, Metropolitan Topeka Airport Authority, the Topeka and Shawnee County Public Library, or Washburn University of Topeka. (Ord. 17226 § 126, 1-27-98. Code 1995 § 2-447.)
