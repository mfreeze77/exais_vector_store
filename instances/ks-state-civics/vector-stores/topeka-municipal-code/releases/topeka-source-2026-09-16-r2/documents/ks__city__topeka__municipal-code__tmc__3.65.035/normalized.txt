(a) The service fee levied on all hotels in the District for calendar year 2026 shall be $1.00 per paid occupied room per night on hotel room rentals.
(b) All revenue from the service fees shall be placed in a special fund, as required by K.S.A. 12-1792 and amendments thereto, and shall be used only for the services identified in TMC 3.65.020 . (Ord. 20618 § 1, 12-9-25.)
