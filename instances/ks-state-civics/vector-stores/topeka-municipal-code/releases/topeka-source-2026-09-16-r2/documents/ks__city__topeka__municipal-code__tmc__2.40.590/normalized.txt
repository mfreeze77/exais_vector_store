If the accused person is found not guilty, judgment shall be rendered immediately. If the accused person is found guilty, sentence shall be imposed and judgment rendered without unreasonable delay. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 62, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-97. Formerly 2.110.590.)
