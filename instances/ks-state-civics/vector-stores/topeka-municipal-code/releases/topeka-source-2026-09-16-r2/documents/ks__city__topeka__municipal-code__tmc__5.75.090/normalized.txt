Any applicant for a permit under this article shall submit to the City Clerk with the application a permit fee as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule. Any applicant for a renewal permit under this article shall submit to the City Clerk with the renewal application a renewal permit fee as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule. (Ord. 20631 § 20, 1-13-26.)
