There is hereby adopted by reference the National Electrical Code, 2023 Edition, and any codified supplements thereto, as promulgated by the National Fire Protection Association, Quincy, Massachusetts. The National Electrical Code, 2023 Edition, is hereby adopted as the electrical code of the City and incorporated in this chapter as if fully set out herein. (Ord. 20440 § 1, 7-11-23.)
State Law References: Adoption by reference authorized, K.S.A. 12-3009 et seq., 12-3301 et seq.
