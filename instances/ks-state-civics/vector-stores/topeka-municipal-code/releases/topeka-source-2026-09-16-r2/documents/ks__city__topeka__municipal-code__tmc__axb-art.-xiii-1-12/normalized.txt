The City shall promptly notify the company in writing (to include a map) of areas newly annexed into or deannexed from the corporate limits of the City, and the company shall update its records for the purpose of payment of franchise fees as soon as reasonably practicable after receiving such notice. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this franchise ordinance, the franchise fees provided for in Section 3 shall not become effective within any area annexed by the City until the beginning of the monthly billing cycle which begins no more than 60 days after the date that the City provides the company with a certified copy of the annexation ordinance, proof of publication as required by law and a map of the City detailing the annexed area. (Ord. 20503 § 12, 7-30-24.)
