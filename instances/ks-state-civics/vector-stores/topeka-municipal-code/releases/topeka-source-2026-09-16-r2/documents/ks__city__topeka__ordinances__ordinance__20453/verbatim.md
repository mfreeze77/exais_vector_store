## (Published in the Topeka Metro News September 18, 2023)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20453

- AN ORDINANCE introduced by Deputy Mayor Dobler and Councilmembers Duncan and  Valdivia-Alcala  concerning  unlawful  camping,  amending  § 9.45.350 and § 9.45.360 of the Topeka Municipal Code.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That  section  9.45.350,  Definitions,  of  The  Code  of  the  City  of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

- Definitions. 12

13

14

15

Unless the particular provisions or the context otherwise requires, the definitions contained  in  this  section  shall  govern  the  construction,  meaning,  and  application  of words and phrases used in this article.

'Camp'  means  to  use  property  for  living  accommodation  purposes,  such  as 16 sleeping activities or making preparations to sleep (including the laying down of bedding 17 for the purpose of sleeping); or storing personal property (including, but not limited to, 18 clothing, sleeping bags, bedrolls, blankets, sheets, luggage, backpacks, kitchen 19 utensils, cookware, and similar material); or making any fire, regularly cooking meals, 20 using any tents, or living in a parked vehicle. These activities constitute camping when it 21 reasonably  appears,  in  light  of  all  the  circumstances,  that  a  person(s)  is  using  such 22 property as a living accommodation. Camping does not include camping on a property 23 by the owner of record for a period not to exceed ten (10) days within any six (6) month 24 period. 25
