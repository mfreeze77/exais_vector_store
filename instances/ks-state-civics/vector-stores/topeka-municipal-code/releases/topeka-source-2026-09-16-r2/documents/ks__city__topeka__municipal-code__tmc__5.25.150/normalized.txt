At any time within 90 days prior to the expiration of a franchise awarded pursuant to this article and at any time after such expiration, termination or surrender of a franchise awarded pursuant to this article, the grantee may apply for an award of a franchise for an additional term, not to exceed 20 years, subject to all the provisions of this article as then in force which are applicable generally to the application for such a franchise. (Code 1981 § 6-33. Code 1995 § 62-52.)
