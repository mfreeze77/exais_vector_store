The Council, in addition to any other authority provided by law, may provide for and charge all or any part of the cost of any work performed by the City which is deemed necessary as a result of the failure of any person to perform any work which has been made such person’s duty to perform by law or ordinance. (Code 1981 § 35-41. Code 1995 § 126-96.)
