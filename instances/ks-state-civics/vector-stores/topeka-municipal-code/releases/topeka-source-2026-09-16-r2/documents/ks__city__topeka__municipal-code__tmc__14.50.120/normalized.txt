Chapter 5, Section 517.8.3, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:
No solid-fuel cooking device of any type shall be permitted for deep-fat frying nor shall any solid-fuel cooking device be permitted within three (3) feet (0.92 m) of any deep-fat frying unit.
(Ord. 20595 § 18, 8-19-25.)
