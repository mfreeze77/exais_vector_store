All meetings of the Committee shall be open to the public and the Committee shall complete its work and submit its report and recommendations within six months of its appointment. Such report shall be delivered to the City’s Governing Body, which may act on said recommendations in a manner it deems appropriate. The Committee’s report format should include, but not be limited to, areas of concern addressed, general findings relative thereto, and specific recommendations for alleviating concerns or problems. (Ord. 20206 § 76, 9-10-19; Ord. 19320 § 7, 10-13-09. Code 1995 § 2-14. Formerly 2.13.050.)
