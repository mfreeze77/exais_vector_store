All persons performing work on a public street shall provide barricades, warning devices, and traffic control devices in accordance with the current City-approved edition of the Manual for Uniform Traffic Control Devices, as well as such other rules, regulations, and restrictions promulgated by the City Engineer. (Ord. 18916 § 5, 6-26-07. Code 1995 § 130-159.)
