(a) Properties that are vacant shall be maintained by the owner in a secure manner so as not to be accessible to unauthorized persons.
(b) A “secure manner” shall include, but not be limited to, the closure and locking of windows, doors, gates and other openings of such size that may allow a child to access the interior of the property or structure. Broken windows, doors, gates, and other openings of such size that may allow a child to access the interior of the property or structure must be repaired. Broken windows shall be secured by reglazing of the window. (Ord. 20464 § 7, 11-21-23.)
