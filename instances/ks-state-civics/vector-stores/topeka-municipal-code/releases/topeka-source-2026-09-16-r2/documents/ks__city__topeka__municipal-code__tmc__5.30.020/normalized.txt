It shall be unlawful for an operator or proprietor to permit any automatic amusement device to be installed, operated or maintained in a place of business, which device:
(a) Has been designated by the Federal government or any department thereof as a gaming device and for which a Federal gaming device license is required; or
(b) Has been designated by the laws of the State as a gambling device. (Code 1981 § 7-17. Code 1995 § 14-27.)
State Law References: Unlawful possession of a gambling device, K.S.A. 21-6408 .
