AN ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20621

introduced by Council Member Neil Dobler, de-annexing a certain tract of land within the City of Topeka, Kansas in accordance with K.S.A. 12-504(b) located at the northeast corner of SW Wanamaker Road and SW 53 rd  Street.

WHEREAS, on February 18, 2025, the Governing Body passed and adopted Ordinance No. 20547 annexing approximately 21.39 acres located at the northeast corner of SW Wanamaker Road and SW 53 rd  Street within Shawnee County, Kansas and adjacent to the City of Topeka corporate limits; and

WHEREAS, the annexation was based on plans for residential development near or adjacent to the annexed property; and

WHEREAS, on September 10, 2025, the Topeka City Council received a letter from Auburn Washburn Unified School District #437 requesting that the City de-annex he previously annexed property due to a change in circumstances leading to the abandonment of the residential development plans to be within the city near or  adjacent to the property; and

WHEREAS, City staff has determined that de-annexation of the property as legally described below is appropriate given the change in circumstances; and

WHEREAS, notice of a public hearing on the petition for de-annexation was published in the official City newspaper, and a public hearing was held on December 16, 2025, all in accordance with the requirements of K.S.A. 12-504 et seq.;  and

WHEREAS, on December 16, 2025, the City Council considered the deannexation petition and hereby enacts this Ordinance, all in accordance with the requirements of K.S.A. 12-504 et seq.

ORD/Deannexation 11/17/2025

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

SECTION 1. Findings. In accordance with K.S.A. 12-505, the City Council finds and concludes that no private rights will be injured or endangered by such exclusion  of land from the corporate boundaries of the City, that the public will not suffer loss  or inconvenience because of such exclusion, and that justice to the owner requires that the petition ought to be granted and the de-annexation of the land be ordered.

SECTION 2. De-annexation. The City Council hereby grants said petition and the following described property is hereby de-annexed from the corporate boundaries of the City of Topeka, Kansas:

And a tract of land in the Southwest Quarter of Section 28, Township 12 South,  Range  15  East  of  the  6th  P.M.  in  Shawnee  County,   Kansas, described as follows; All of the West Half of the Southwest Quarter of   the said Southwest Quarter of Section 28. The above described tract  contains 20.12 acres, more or less.

SECTION 3. Recording. The City Clerk shall deliver a certified copy of this Ordinance to the Shawnee County Register of Deeds so that the de-annexation is properly recorded and the excluded land is designated as no longer being within  the corporate boundaries of the City of Topeka, Kansas.

SECTION 4. All ordinances or parts or sections of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed except that the 1.27 acres of right-of-way annexed by Ordinance No. 20547 shall remain within the boundaries of the City of Topeka, Kansas.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall take effect and be in full force and effect following its adoption and publication as provided by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body on December 16, 2025.

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS

Michael A. Padilla, Mayor
