(a) Permit Required. It shall be unlawful for any person to conduct a special event without a special event permit issued by the City Manager or designee.
(b) Exceptions. A special event permit is not required for the following activities:
(1) Special events conducted by the City;
(2) Funeral processions;
(3) Processions or marches conducted entirely on sidewalks where the participants comply with traffic regulations and traffic control devices. (Ord. 20038 § 16, 12-6-16.)
