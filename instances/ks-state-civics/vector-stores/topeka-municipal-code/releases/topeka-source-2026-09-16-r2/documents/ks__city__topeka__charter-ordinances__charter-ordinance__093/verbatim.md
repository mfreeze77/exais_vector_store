## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

- Section 1. That City of Topeka Charter Ordinance; Topeka City Code Section A216, Composition and eligibility is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Sec. A2-16. Composition and eligibility.

- (a) Composition. The governing body shall be a nine-member council, hereinafter referred to as the "council,;" and a mayor nominated and elected at large on a nonpartisan basis. The nine councilmembers shall be nominated and elected from nine districts of near equal population on a nonpartisan basis. Each council district shall be represented by one district councilmember.
- (b) [Residency requirements ] The mayor and each district councilmember shall be a citizen of the United States and a qualified elector of Topeka; Kansas and must reside in the city at the time of filing for election and thereafter for the duration of their terms. Councilmembers from districts shall be residents of their respective districts at the time of filing for election and thereafter for the duration of their terms.
- (c) Eligibility. person   desiring to become a candidate for mayor or councilmember shall file with the city clerk, or such appointive officer as the council may designate; before the filing deadline a statement of such candidacy on a form furnished by Any
