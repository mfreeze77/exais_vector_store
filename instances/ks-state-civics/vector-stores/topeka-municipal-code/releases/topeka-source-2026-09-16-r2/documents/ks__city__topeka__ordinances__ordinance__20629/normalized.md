## ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20629

introduced  by    City    Manager  Dr.    Robert    M.    Perez    amending  the 'District  Map' referred to and made a part of the Zoning Ordinances by Section 18.50.050 of the Topeka Municipal Code on property located at 2012  SW  10 th  Avenue  from  'C-2'  Commercial  District  TO  'PUD' Planned Unit Development District (limited C-2 Commercial District uses plus  one  to  two  residential  dwelling  units  on  the  ground  floor).    (PUD 25/13) (Council District No. 1)

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That the 'District Map' referred to and made a part of the Zoning Ordinances by

Section 18.50.050 of the Topeka Municipal Code (TMC), be, and the same is hereby amended, by reclassifying the following described property:

LOTS 462-463, MELROSE SUBDIVISION

FROM 'C-2' Commercial District TO 'PUD' Planned Unit Development (limited C-2 Commercial District uses plus one to two residential dwelling units on the ground floor). Subject to:

1. 'C-2' Commercial District uses are permitted except as noted by the conditions herein. Additionally, a single or two-family dwelling in the existing building is permitted. The existing building may include a C-2 use and single family or twofamily dwelling.
2. The following C-2 uses are not permitted:
- a. Automobile or Vehicle Carwash
- b. Auto Service Types I, II, or III
- c. Drive-through Establishments
- d. Automobile Rental Establishments for passenger cars and  trucks
3. The use and dimensional standards applicable to C-2 zoning shall apply except as stated in these conditions. The existing building does not meet minimum required building setbacks.  Any existing building setbacks that do not conform to dimensional standards of either C-2 or 'R' zoning are deemed to be allowed.  Applicable building codes may require a larger setback than the current building setbacks.
4. The intent of this Planned Unit Development zoning is to facilitate productive use of the existing building. In the event that the building is destroyed by a fire or other event, or  is  demolished  by  the  owner,  any  new  building  shall  be  in  the  same  location  and within the footprint of the existing building and not exceed a single story.
5. Any change to the allowed uses and conditions herein will require an amendment to the Planned Unit Development in accordance with the amendment procedures in TMC 18.190.070.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body, City of Topeka, Kansas on January 13, 2026.
