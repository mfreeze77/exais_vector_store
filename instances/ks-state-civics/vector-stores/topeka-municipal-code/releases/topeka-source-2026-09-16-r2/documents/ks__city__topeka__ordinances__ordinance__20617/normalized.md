(Published in the Topeka Metro News December 15, 2025)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20617

AN ORDINANCE INTRODUCED BY CITY MANAGER DR. ROBERT M. PEREZ, ESTABLISHING A SUNFLOWER CROSSING COMMUNITY IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT (CID) AND LEVYING A CID SALES TAX OF ONE PERCENT (1%) WITHIN THE CID.

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Community Improvement District Act, K.S.A. 12- 6a26 through 126a36, as amended (the 'Act') cities are authorized to establish Community Improvement Districts ('CID') for economic development purposes; and

WHEREAS, the City of Topeka, Kansas (the 'City') received a petition from the owners of record of more than 55% of the land within a proposed CID district ('District') generally described as Sunflower Crossing and the persons collectively owning more than 55% by assessed value of the land area within the proposed District; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 9729, the City has provided notice that it would hold a public hearing on December 9, 2025, to consider establishing a CID and make findings necessary therefore; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Council (the 'Governing Body') conducted a public hearing on December 9, 2025, to consider establishing the proposed District, all in accordance with the Act; and

WHEREAS, the Act provides that upon the conclusion of the public hearing the Governing Body may authorize the CID and project therein.

## THAT: BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS,

- Section 1. Nature of the Project. The general nature of the proposed project (the  'CID Project')  to  be  undertaken  within  the  District  is  demolition,  site  work,  and  infrastructure improvements to promote redevelopment within the District, which is generally located northwest of the intersection of S.W. Topeka Boulevard and S. Kansas Avenue within the City.
- Section  2.  Findings. The  Governing  Body  finds  the  District  furthers  economic  development within the City and further finds it advisable and in the City's best interest to establish the District and authorize the CID Project as proposed, subject to a development agreement executed by the City Manager (the 'Development Agreement').
- Section 3.      Estimated Project Cost. The estimated cost of the CID Project within the District is approximately $971,674, plus associated interest and other financing costs

Section 4. Legal Description. The legal description of the District is:

An area including the following additions, lots and parcels all located in Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas:

Lots 1, 2, 3, and 5, Block A, Sunflower Crossing Subdivision, a subdivision of land in the City of Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas and all streets, roads, highways, and public rightof-way contiguous to the aforementioned property.

Section 5.          Map. A map of the District is attached as Exhibit A.

Section  6.  Method  of  Financing;  Sales  Tax. The  method  of  financing  the  CID  Project  is through a CID sales tax. The proposed amount of the CID sales tax to be levied is one percent (1%) which shall  be levied upon all sales of tangible personal property at retail or services taxable within the CID District pursuant to the Kansas Retailers' Sales Tax Act. There are proposed to be no special assessments on property within the boundaries of the CID district.

Section  7.      Sales  Tax  Levy. The City authorizes and hereby levies a one percent (1%) CID sales tax as described in Section 6 which shall expire no later than 22 years from the date the state director of taxation begins collecting the tax, which is scheduled to commence on January 1, 2027, or when the payas-you-go eligible CID Project costs have been paid, subject to the Development Agreement.

Section 8.        Effective Date. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect on from and after its passage by a majority of the Governing Body and publication of it or a summary thereof once in the official City newspaper.

Section 9.     Recording. After publication, the City Clerk is hereby directed to file a copy of this ordinance with the Shawnee County Register of Deeds.

Section  10.  Kansas  Department  of  Revenue. The  City  Clerk  will  provide  the  Kansas Department  of  Revenue  with  a  certified  copy  of  this  ordinance  notifying  the  Department  of  the establishment of the District and the levy of the CID sales tax to commence at a date indicated in the Development Agreement.
