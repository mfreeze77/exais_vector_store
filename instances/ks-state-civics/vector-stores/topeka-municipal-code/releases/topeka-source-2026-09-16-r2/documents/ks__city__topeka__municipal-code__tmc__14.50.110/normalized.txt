Chapter 5, Section 517.7, Fire-Extinguishing Equipment for Solid-Fuel Cooking, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:
Fire-extinguishing equipment for solid-fuel cooking requirements shall be governed by the currently adopted Fire Code of the City of Topeka.
(Ord. 20595 § 17, 8-19-25; Ord. 19216 § 16, 2-3-09. Code 1995 § 26-503.10. Formerly 14.50.190.)
