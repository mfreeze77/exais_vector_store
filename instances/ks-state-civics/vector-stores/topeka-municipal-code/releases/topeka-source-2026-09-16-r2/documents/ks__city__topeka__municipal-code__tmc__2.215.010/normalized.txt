(a) That in accordance with the provisions of K.S.A. 12-1757 et seq. (the “Act”), a public building commission to be known as the Topeka Public Building Commission (the “Commission”) be, and the same is, hereby created.
(b) The Commission shall have all the powers and authority granted to public building commissions under the Act, not inconsistent with the provisions contained herein. (Ord. 20206 § 82, 9-10-19; Ord. 17808 § 1, 3-26-02; Ord. 17237 § 1, 2-24-98. Code 1995 § 26-66. Formerly 2.70.010.)
