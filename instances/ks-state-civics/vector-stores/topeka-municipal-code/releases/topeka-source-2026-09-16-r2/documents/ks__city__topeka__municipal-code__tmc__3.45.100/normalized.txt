A service assessment made under this chapter shall be made by ordinance after a statement of the costs has been given the owner or other person and not less than 60 days thereafter, during which time the charge may be paid without interest to the City Treasurer. All such money shall be credited as a reimbursable item to the fund of the City from which the costs of such services by the City are paid or payable. (Code 1981 § 35-42. Code 1995 § 126-97.)
