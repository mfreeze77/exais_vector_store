Water used by all City departments and divisions from a fire hydrant shall be measured by a method acceptable to the Utilities Director. The Fire Department shall submit a report monthly to the Utilities Director stating estimated water usage for firefighting. All other City divisions or departments shall report monthly to the Utilities Director the amount of water used by their department or division. (Ord. 20089 § 20, 10-17-17.)
