705.8.1.1, ETCO “T” Cone and Couple Adapters, is hereby created by the addition of the following provisions:
The use of this type of clay tile to plastic adapter is limited to sanitary sewer installations and may only be used for the connection of the building sewer to the clay tile sewer main wye or tap. The joint and connecting pipe shall be installed at no more than twenty-two (22) degrees from the horizontal and shall be laid on undisturbed ground or a firm bed of one-quarter (1/4) inch crushed rock or similar material. The crushed rock shall be no less than one-quarter (1/4) inch nor greater than three-quarter (3/4) inch.
(Ord. 20570 § 30, 7-1-25; Ord. 20257 § 34, 8-18-20. Formerly 14.35.310.)
