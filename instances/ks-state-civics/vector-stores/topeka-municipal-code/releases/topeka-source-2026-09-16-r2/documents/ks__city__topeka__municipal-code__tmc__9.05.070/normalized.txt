(a) It shall be unlawful for any person to sell or resell in the City or at Heartland Motorsports Park any ticket or other evidence of right of entry to any public amusement event or sporting event at a price more than the maximum price printed on the face of such ticket or other evidence of the right of entry.
(b) Within the meaning of this section, a public amusement event shall be deemed to include any event designed, performed or conducted in the City or at Heartland Motorsports Park for the purpose of public amusement, entertainment, exhibition, contest or performance.
(c) Any person violating any provision of this section shall be prosecuted in Municipal Court. (Ord. 16799 § 1, 2-14-95. Code 1995 § 54-7.)
