Section 2. Section  A2-30,  Council  rules  of  procedure,  as  established  by Charter Ordinance No. 110, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Council rules of procedure.

(a) Meetings. The Council shall meet regularly at least twice in every month at 31 times and places as the Council may prescribe by ordinary ordinance. Special meetings 32 may be held on the call of the Mayor or of four (4) or more district Councilmembers in 33 the manner prescribed by rules of the Council. 34

(b) Rules and journal. The Council shall determine its own rules and order of 35 business and shall provide for keeping a journal of its proceedings. 36

(c) Voting.  Five  (5)  members  of  the  Council  shall  constitute  a  quorum.  All 37 actions by the Council shall be taken by the affirmative vote of five (5) or more members 38 of  the  Council, unless a greater or lesser number of votes is required by this Charter 39 Ordinance, by the Statutes of Kansas, or by the Constitution of Kansas. The votes of all 40 Councilmembers shall be recorded in the journal. 41

(d) Form. Proposed ordinary ordinances and resolutions shall be introduced 42 in the Council only in written or printed form. No ordinary ordinance shall contain more 43 than one subject which shall be clearly expressed in its title. The enacting clause of all 44 ordinary  ordinances  shall  be:  'Be  it  Ordained  by  the  Council  of  the  City  of  Topeka, 45 Kansas:'.  Any  ordinary  ordinance  which  repeals  or  amends  an  existing  ordinary 46 ordinance or part of the Code of Ordinances of the city shall set out in full the sections 47 or subsections to be repealed or amended, and shall indicate matter to be omitted by 48 enclosing  it in  brackets  or  by  strikeout  type  and  shall  indicate  new  matter  by 49 underscoring or by italics. 50

(e) Procedure.  Except  in  the  case  of  emergency  ordinances,  and  except where  Kansas  Statutes  or  other  applicable  law  provides  a  different  procedure  for passage of ordinances for a specific purpose, every proposed ordinary ordinance shall be read by title in open a Council meeting two (2) times before final passage, and at least one (1) week shall elapse between introduction and final passageAll ordinances shall be considered at a Council meeting. A copy of each proposed ordinary ordinance shall  be  provided  for  each  district  Councilmember  and  the  Mayor  at  the  time  of  its introduction, and at least three (3) copies shall be provided for public inspection in the office of the City Clerk or such other city officer as the Council may designate until it is finally adopted or fails of adoption. Persons interested in a proposed ordinary ordinance shall be given an opportunity to be heard before the Council in accordance with such rules and regulations as the Council may adopt. If the Council adopts an amendment to a proposed ordinary ordinance which constitutes a change in substance, the proposed ordinary ordinance as amended shall be placed on file for public inspection in the office of the City Clerk or such other appointive officer as the Council may designate for one (1) additional week before final passage.

(f) Emergency  ordinances.  An  ordinary  ordinance  may  be  passed  as  an emergency measure on the day of its introduction if it contains a declaration describing in clear and specific terms the facts and reasons constituting the emergency. Neither an ordinance granting, reviewing, or extending a franchise, nor a Charter Ordinance, shall be passed as an emergency ordinance.

- (g) Publication. The City Clerk or such other city officer as the Council may 72 designate  shall  cause  all  ordinary  ordinances,  including  but  not  limited  to  emergency 73

ordinances,  as  soon  as  practicable  after  they  have  been  passed  and  signed,  to  be 74 published  once  in  the  official  city  newspaper,  unless  Kansas  Statutes  or  other 75 applicable law require more publications. In lieu of publication of the entire ordinance, 76 the  City  Clerk  may  publish  a  summary  of  the  ordinance  provided  all  of  the  following 77 requirements are met: 78

(1) The  publication  is  identified  as  a  'summary'  and  contains  notice 79 that the complete text of the ordinance may be obtained or viewed free of charge 80 at the City Clerk's office or on the city's website. 81

(2) The  City  Attorney  certifies  the  summary  of  the  ordinance  prior  to 82 publication to ensure that the summary is legally accurate and sufficient. 83

(3) The publication contains the city's official website address where a 84 reproduction  of  the  original  ordinance  is  available  for  at  least  ninety  days 85 following the summary publication in the newspaper. 86

(4) If  an  ordinance  is  subject  to  a  petition  pursuant  to  state  law,  the 87 summary shall contain a statement to that effect. 88

(h) Effective date. Except in the case of emergency ordinances, and except 89 where  Kansas  Statutes  otherwise  specify,  every  adopted  ordinary  ordinance  shall 90 become effective  upon  its  publication  pursuant  to  subsection  (g)  or  at  any  later  date 91 specified therein. Emergency ordinances shall become effective upon passage or at any 92 later date specified therein. 93

## Duties. 103

The City Manager shall have the power and it shall be his or her duty: 104

(a) To  execute  and  enforce  all  laws  and  ordinances  and  policies  of  the 105 Council and to administer the affairs of the city. 106
