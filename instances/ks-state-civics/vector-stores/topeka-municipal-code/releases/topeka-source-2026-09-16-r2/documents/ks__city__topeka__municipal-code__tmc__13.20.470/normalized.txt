Permits issued under this article shall be for a specified time period, not to exceed five years. A permit may be issued for a period less than a year or may be stated to expire on a specific date. The user shall apply for permit reissuance a minimum of 45 days prior to the expiration of the user’s existing permit. The terms and conditions of the permit may be subject to modification by the Utilities Director during the term of the permit as limitations or requirements are modified or other just cause exists. The user shall be informed of any proposed changes in his permit at least 30 days prior to the effective date of change. Any changes or new conditions in the permit shall include a reasonable time schedule for compliance. (Ord. 20089 § 69, 10-17-17.)
