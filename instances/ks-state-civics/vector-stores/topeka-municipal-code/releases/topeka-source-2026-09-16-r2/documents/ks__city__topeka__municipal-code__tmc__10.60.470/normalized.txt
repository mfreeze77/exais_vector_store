The Traffic Engineer shall designate and establish zones or areas on the public streets for the stopping of buses for the safe and convenient loading and unloading of passengers and may designate, establish and maintain taxicab stands on the public streets in the manner and under the conditions expressed in this article. (Code 1981 § 43-495. Code 1995 § 142-501.)
