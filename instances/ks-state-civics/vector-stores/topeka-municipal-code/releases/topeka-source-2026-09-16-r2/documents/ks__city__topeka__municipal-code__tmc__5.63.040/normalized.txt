All licenses and registrations shall be for a term of two years and shall expire the last working day before December 15th in the second year of the term. In the event of the expiration of a license or registration, no new license or registration shall be issued until one year after the license or registration has expired. In the event the individual elects to be licensed during this one-year period, he or she will be required to pay the renewal fee, plus applicable late fees, or other fees as required under the code.
In December of 2024, all individuals who apply for or renew their licenses and registrations shall be divided into two groups as assigned by the Development Services Director or designee. The first group of individuals shall pay the applicable biennial fees and apply for or renew their licenses and registrations for a term of two years, that shall expire on the last working day before December 15th in even-numbered years. The second group of individuals shall pay the applicable annual fees and apply for or renew their licenses and registrations for a term of one year that shall expire on the last working day before December 15, 2025. After December 15, 2025, the second group of individuals shall pay the applicable biennial fees and apply for or renew their licenses and registrations for a term of two years that shall expire on the last working day before December 15th in odd-numbered years. (Ord. 20524 § 1, 10-1-24; Ord. 19330 § 23, 11-3-09. Code 1995 § 30-523.)
