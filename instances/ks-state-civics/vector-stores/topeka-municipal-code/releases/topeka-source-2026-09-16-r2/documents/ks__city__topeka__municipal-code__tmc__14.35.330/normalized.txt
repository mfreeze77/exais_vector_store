1001.1.1, Domestic Kitchen Sink Exception, is hereby created by the addition of the following provisions:
Not more than one (1) trap shall be permitted on a trap arm; provided, however, a separate trap may be installed on each compartment of a domestic kitchen sink with the waste discharging into a single trap arm.
(Ord. 20570 § 37, 7-1-25; Ord. 19829 § 39, 7-9-13. Formerly 14.35.380.)
