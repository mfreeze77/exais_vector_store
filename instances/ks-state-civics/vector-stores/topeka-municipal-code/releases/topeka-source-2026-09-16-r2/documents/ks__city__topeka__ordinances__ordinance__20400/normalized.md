Section 9. That section 3.30.460, Affirmative action programs, of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Affirmative action programs.

(a) Submission of Program. All persons seeking to enter into a contract with the City  shall  submit  in  writing  to  the  Contracts  and  Procurement  Division  either  an affirmative  action  program,  a  certificate  of  compliance,  or  such  other  certificate  as  is acceptable to the Contracts and Procurement Division which evidences the adoption of an affirmative action program. Such affirmative action program, certificate of compliance or other certificate shall be approved and on file with the Contracts and Procurement Division, or such plan shall be submitted with the contract bid. If no affirmative action plan is submitted with the contract bid, the bid will be considered nonresponsive and will not  be  accepted.  If  any  person  shall  fail  or  refuse  to  submit  an  affirmative  action program as required by this article, such person shall be ineligible to enter into any City contract until the person has so complied.
