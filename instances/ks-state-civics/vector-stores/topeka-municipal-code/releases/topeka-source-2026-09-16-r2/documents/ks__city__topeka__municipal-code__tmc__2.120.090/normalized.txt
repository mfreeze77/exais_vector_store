The Civil Service Commission shall certify the names and addresses of eligible applicants to the Fire Chief or Police Chief, whichever is applicable, who shall make appointments only from such list so certified. Whenever the eligible list of the Civil Service Commission contains fewer than double the number of applicants to fill the vacancy or vacancies existing, the Personnel Division shall advertise for applicants and the examination procedure shall commence as soon as practical thereafter. The Civil Service Commission shall document why a candidate is or is not certified. What documentation is required will be decided by the City Attorney and the technical advisor. The Personnel Director shall provide whatever support is appropriate to provide documentation. (Ord. 20206 §§ 28, 86, 9-10-19.)
