In all cases a complaint and notice to appear in Municipal Court may be made in the form of a complaint and notice to appear which shall be deemed sufficient if it contains the information required by subsection (b) of K.S.A. 8-2106 , and amendments thereto. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 21, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-56. Formerly 2.110.190.)
