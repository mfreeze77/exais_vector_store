## ORDINANCE

(Published in the Topeka Metro News September 18, 2023)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20451

introduced  by  Interim  City  Manager  Richard  U.  Nienstedt  relating  to  the  partial vacation of a 16' wide sanitary sewer easement located on Lot 1, Block A, Helping Hands Humane Society Subdivision at  2625 NW Rochester Road to allow for  a building expansion and alignment of the sanitary service line serving the building addition (V23E/02) (Council District No. 2) .

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That pursuant to the requirements of K.S.A. 12-504 as amended, a petition file by Cromwell, Michael L and Elizabeth F., as property owners, has been filed with the office of the City Clerk, requesting the vacation of the following described portion of public sanitary sewer easement located within the City of Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas:

Section 2. That  said  petition  has  been  duly  published  for  two  (2)  consecutive  weeks  in  a newspaper of general circulation and on September 12, 2023 , the petition is ready for determination by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, Kansas.
