No trafficway capital improvement shall be commenced if, within 20 days after publication of the ordinance ordering the trafficway capital improvement, written protests signed by both 51 percent or more of the resident owners of record of property within the trafficway benefit area and the owners of record of more than one-half of the total area of such area are filed with the City Clerk. (Ord. 18273 § 12, 6-22-04. Code 1995 § 130-771.)
