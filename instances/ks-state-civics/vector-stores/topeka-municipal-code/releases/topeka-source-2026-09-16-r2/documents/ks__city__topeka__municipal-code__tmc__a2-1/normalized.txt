The City of Topeka, Kansas, by the power vested in it by article 12 , section 5 , of the Constitution of the State of Kansas, hereby elects to exempt itself from and make inapplicable to it the provisions of K.S.A. 13-103 , 13-502 , 13-506 through 13-516 , 13-518 , 13-527 , 13-907 , 13-1011 , 13-1013 , 13-1410 , and provide substitute and additional provisions as hereinafter set forth in this Charter Ordinance. Such referenced provisions are either enactments or a part thereof which are applicable to this city but are not applicable uniformly to all cities. (C.O. 117 § 1, 2-9-16.)
