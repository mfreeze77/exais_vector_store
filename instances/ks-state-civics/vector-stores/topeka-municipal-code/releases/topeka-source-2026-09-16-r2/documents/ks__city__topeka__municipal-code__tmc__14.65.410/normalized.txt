“Code Official” means the Chief of Police or designee assigned to enforce the International Property Maintenance Code adopted in TMC 8.60.010 .
“Dangerous tree” means any tree which the Code Official determines is in such a condition and is located in such a place as to constitute a danger to persons or property in the vicinity of the tree.
“Owner” means the owner of a mobile home park as defined in TMC 14.65.010 .
“Tree” means a tall woody perennial plant, typically having a single stem or trunk and bearing lateral branches. (Ord. 20502 § 1, 7-30-24.)
