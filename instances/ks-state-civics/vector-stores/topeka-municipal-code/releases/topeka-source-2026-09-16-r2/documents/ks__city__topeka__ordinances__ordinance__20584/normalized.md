(Published in the Topeka Metro News August 18, 2025)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20584

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez adopting a Redevelopment Project Plan for the Topeka Boulevard Tax Increment Financing (TIF) District pursuant to K.S.A. 12-1770 et seq., as amended.

AN ORDINANCE

WHEREAS, on July 15, 2025, the Governing Body adopted Ordinance No. 20576 that established 30 th &amp; Topeka Boulevard Tax Increment Finance District ('District'); and

WHEREAS, the City is now considering the adoption of a redevelopment Project which  provides  for  the  redevelopment of  the

Plan for the  District  (the 'Project Plan'), Project Area within the District; and

WHEREAS, on June 16, 2025, the Planning Commission made a finding that  the Project  Plan  is  consistent  with  the  intent  of  the  City's  comprehensive  plan  for the development of the City; and

WHEREAS, a  copy of  the  Project  Plan  was delivered  to  the Board of County Commissioners of  Shawnee  County and to the Board of  Education of  Unified   School District No. 501; and

WHEREAS, on June 17, 2025, the Governing Body passed Resolution No.  9681 calling a public hearing for consideration of the Project Plan to be held on August 12, 2025; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body conducted the public hearing on August 12, 2025, in the City Council Chambers of City Hall, 214 E. 8 th   Street, at 6:00 p.m., as required  by K.S.A. 12-1770 et seq. , as amended.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. The Governing Body adopts the Project Plan which is attached hereto as Exhibit A and specifically incorporated herein.

Section 2. That any substantial changes as defined in K.S.A. 12-1770a, and amendments  thereto,  to  the  Project  Plan  shall  be  subject  to  a  public  hearing and publication of notice in accordance with K.S.A. 12-1770, et seq., and amendments thereto.

Section 3. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage and approval by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Governing Body, and publication in the official City newspaper.

PASSED and APPROVED by the Governing Body August 12, 2025.

ATTEST:
