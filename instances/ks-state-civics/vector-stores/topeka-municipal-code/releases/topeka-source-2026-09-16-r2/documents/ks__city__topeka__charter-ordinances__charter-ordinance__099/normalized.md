Section 3. That Section 4 of Charter Ordinance No. 23, as amended by Charter 52 53  Ordinance No. 52 § 2, codified as § A9-18, Transit board-Creation; compensation, of The 54  Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Transit board--Creation; compensation.

The governing and administrative body of the authority shall be a board consisting 56 57  of fiveseven (7) members, to be known as the transit board.  Members of the board shall 58  be residents of the metropolitan area and individuals of recognized business ability.

Each member of the initial board shall be compensated at the rate of $20.00 per 59 60  diem for each day or part thereof spent in meetings of the board, not to exceed $500.00 in 61  any calendar year. The compensation of successor members of the board shall be fixed by 62  the  board.  No  board  member  shall  be  allowed  any  fees,  prerequisites  or  emoluments, 63  reward  or  compensation  for  his  or  her  services  as  a  member  or  officer  of  the  authority aside  from  his  or  her  salary,  but  the  individual  shall  be  reimbursed  for  actual  expenses 64 65  incurred by the individual in the performance of his or her duties.

## Transit board-Appointment of board; terms.

Appointment.  Members of the board shall be appointed and may be removed in the 69 70  manner specified in Charter Ordinance No. 94, Section 24, as codified at Section A2-72.

Terms.  The term of any board member shall be for four years from the date of his 71 72  or  her  appointment.  Members shall not serve beyond the end of their appointed terms. 73  Upon  expiration  of  a  term,  the  position  will  remain  vacant  until  such  a  successor  is 74  appointed.    Vacancies  shall  be  filled  for  any  unexpired  term  in  the  same  manner  as

appointments.    The  city  clerk  shall  certify  the  action  of  the  council  with  respect  to  such 75 76  appointments and file such certificates as part of the records of the office of the city clerk.
