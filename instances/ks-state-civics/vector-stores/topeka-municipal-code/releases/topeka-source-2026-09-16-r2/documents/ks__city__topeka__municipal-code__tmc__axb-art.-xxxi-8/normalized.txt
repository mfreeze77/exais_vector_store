(a) During the term of this Contract Franchise, MCImetro shall obtain and maintain insurance coverage at its sole expense, with financially reputable insurers that are licensed, authorized or permitted to do business in the State of Kansas. Should MCImetro elect to use the services of an affiliated captive insurance company for this purpose, that company shall possess a certificate of authority from the Kansas Insurance Commissioner. MCImetro shall provide the following insurance:
(1) Workers' compensation in compliance with the statutory requirements of the state(s) of operation and employers' liability with limits of $1,000,000 each accident/disease/policy limit or the limit equal to the amount required bylaw.
(2) Commercial general liability with limits of Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence for bodily injury and property damage and Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) general aggregate including coverage for contractual liability and products completed operations liability. Upon request, the City shall be included as an additional insured as their interest may appear under this Agreement with respect to liability arising from MCImetro's operations under this Contract Franchise. (Ord. 20628 § 8, 1-13-26.)
