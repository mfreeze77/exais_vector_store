## ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20381

introduced by City Manager Stephen M. Wade relating to the vacation of the south 8' of a public  utility  easement  along  the  north  side  of  property  at  2880  SE  California  Avenue, excepting out the 8' X 16' portion needed for maintenance and repair of the City public asset. (Council District #4)

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That pursuant to the requirements of K.S.A. 12-504 as amended, a petition by McDonald's Corporation, as property owner, has been filed with the office of the City Clerk, requesting the vacation of the following described public easement located within the City of Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas:

## LEGAL DESCRIPTION

THE NORTH 8 FEET OF LOT 1 BLOCK A PARK VALLEY SUBDIVISION NO. 5 TO THE CITY OF TOPEKA , SHAWNEE COUNTY, KANSAS;

LESS AND EXCEPT THE FOLLOWING DESCRIBED TRACT;

COMMENCING AT THE NORTHWEST CORNER OF SAID LOT 1; THENCE ON BEARING OF N88*32'22"E ALONG THE NOR TH LINE OF SAID LOT 1, DISTANCE OF 125.04 FEET TO THE POINT OF BEGINNING; THENCE CONTINUING N88*32'22"E ALONG SAID NORTH LINE, A DISTANCE OF

16.01 FEET;

THENCE 500*03'58"W 4 DISTANCE OF 8.00 FEET;

THENCE 588*32'22"W DISTANCE OF 16.01 FEET;

THENCE 4 DISTANCE OF 8.00 FEET TO THE POINT OF BEGINNING.

CONTAINS 2,352 SQUARE FEET, 0.054 ACRES, MORE OR LESS.

Section 2. That said petition has been duly published for two (2) consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation and on November 1, 2022 , the petition is ready for determination by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, Kansas.

Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, after being duly informed and hearing the evidence presented finds that:

- a. Legal notice was given as required by K.S.A. 12-504 as amended.
- b. No private rights will be injured or endangered by such vacation.
- c. The public will suffer no loss or inconvenience by such vacation.

Section 4. That the Governing Body of the City of Topeka does hereby find that justice requires the petition of vacation be granted and does hereby order the vacation of the below described public easement located within the City of Topeka, Kansas:

## LEGAL DESCRIPTION

THE NORTH FEET OF LOT 1 BLOCK A, PARK VALLEY SUBDIVISION NO. 5 TO THE CITY OF TOPEKA, SHAWNEE COUNTY, KANSAS;

LESS AND EXCEPT THE FOLLOWING DESCRIBED TRACT;

COMMENCING AT THE NORTHWEST CORNER OF SAID LOT 1; THENCE ON AN ASSUMED BEARING OF N88*32'22"E ALONG THE NOR TH LINE OF SAID LOT 1, A DISTANCE OF 125.04 FEET TO THE POINT OF BEGINNING; THENCE CONTINUING N88*32'22"E ALONG SAID NORTH LINE , A DISTANCE OF 16.01 FEET;

THENCE 500*03'58"w A DISTANCE OF 8.00 FEET;

THENCE 4 DISTANCE OF 8.00 FEET TO THE POINT OF BEGINNING.

THENCE 588*32'22"w DISTANCE OF 16.01 FEET;

CONTAINS 2,352 SQUARE FEET, MORE OR LESS.

Section 5. The City Clerk is hereby directed to certify a copy of this ordinance to the Shawnee County Register of Deeds Office for appropriate recording.

Section 6 . This  ordinance  shall  take  effect  and  be  in  force  from  and  after  its  passage,  approval  and publication in the official city newspaper.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka November 1, 2022.
