(a) It shall be unlawful to rent space or allocate space to flea market sellers without a flea market promoter license issued pursuant to this chapter.
(b) A flea market promoter providing space to flea market sellers at more than one location shall be required to obtain a license for each location. Only one license per location shall be required for each flea market and individual flea market sellers shall not be required to obtain any other City license in order to operate at a flea market. (Ord. 20039 § 13, 12-6-16.)
