All water consumed from a fire hydrant located within the City shall be billed at the established inside City commercial rate. All water consumed from a fire hydrant located outside the City shall be billed at the established outside City commercial rate. The Fire Department shall not be billed for any water consumed from a fire hydrant for firefighting or fire training purposes. (Ord. 19300 § 4, 8-25-09. Code 1995 § 146-20.)
