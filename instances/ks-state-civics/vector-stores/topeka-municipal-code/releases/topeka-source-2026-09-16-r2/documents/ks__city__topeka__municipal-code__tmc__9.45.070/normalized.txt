(a) It shall be unlawful for any person to be found lurking, lying in wait or concealed in any house or other building or any yard, premises or street with the intent to do any mischief or to pilfer or to commit any crime or misdemeanor whatever.
(b) It shall be unlawful for any person to loiter at nighttime or prowl around a dwelling house or any other place used wholly or in part for living or dwelling purposes, belonging to or occupied by another. This subsection applies only when a person is on another’s property without permission. (Code 1981 § 15-71. Code 1995 § 54-128.)
