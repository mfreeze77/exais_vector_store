A fee schedule may be established by resolution, duly passed by the Governing Body, for various licenses, applications, permits and other city fees and charges as identified in the Topeka Municipal Code to be prescribed by the City’s fee schedule. The fee schedule may be amended from time to time by resolution of the Governing Body. A copy of the fee schedule shall be filed with the City Clerk to be open to inspection and available to the public at all reasonable hours. (Ord. 20631 § 1, 1-13-26.)
