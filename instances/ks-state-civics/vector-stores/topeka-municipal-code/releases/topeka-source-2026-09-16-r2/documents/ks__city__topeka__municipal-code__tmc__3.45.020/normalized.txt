Before any contract is let or any work is authorized for any public improvement authorized by the laws of the State or ordinances of the City whereby a special benefit district therefor will be created and taxes levied therefor, the Council shall by resolution direct and order a public hearing on the advisability of such improvement. Notice of such hearing shall be in such manner and form as provided in this chapter. (Code 1981 § 35-18. Code 1995 § 126-36.)
