Only upon written approval of the Governing Body of the City of Topeka may the rights and obligations of EMS pursuant to this franchise ordinance be transferred to a person meeting the requirements for an ambulance service, as determined by the laws and regulations of the State and the City at the time of the contemplated transfer. Any approved transferee shall, without limitation, assume all the duties and obligations of EMS and EMS shall be released of all future rights, duties, and obligations arising from this franchise ordinance. (Ord. 20561 § 12, 5-20-25.)
