## (Published in the Topeka Metro News October 7, 2024)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20524

AN ORDINANCE

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez, amending Sections 5.63.040 and 5.63.050 concerning licenses and registration term and fees of the Topeka Municipal Code and repealing original sections.

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That section 5.63.040, Licenses and Registration term, of the Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Licenses and registrations term.

All licenses and registrations shall be for a term of one two (2) years and shall expire the last working day before December 15 th  in the second year of the term. In the event of the expiration of a license or registration, no new license or registration shall be issued  until  one  year  after  the  license  or  registration  has  expired.  In  the  event  the individual elects to be licensed during this one-year period, he or she will be required to pay the renewal fee, plus an applicable late fees, or other fees as required under the Code.

In  December of 2024, all individuals who apply for or renew their licenses and registrations shall be divided into two groups as assigned by the Development Services Director or designee. The first group of individuals shall pay the applicable biennial fees and apply for or renew their licenses and registrations for a term of two years, that shall expire  on  the  last  working  day  before  December  15 th   in  even-numbered  years.  The second group of individuals shall pay the applicable annual fees and apply for or renew their licenses and registrations for a term of one year that shall expire on the last working day before December 15, 2025. Section 3. This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage, approval and publication in the official City newspaper.

Section 4. This ordinance shall supersede all ordinances, resolutions or rules, or portions thereof, which are in conflict with the provisions of this ordinance.
