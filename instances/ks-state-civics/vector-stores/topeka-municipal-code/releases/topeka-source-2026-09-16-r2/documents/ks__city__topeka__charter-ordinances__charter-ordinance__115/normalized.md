## CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 115

A CHARTER ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Jim Colson amending Charter Ordinance codified at TMC Section A9-2 concerning the mill levy for the Topeka Metropolitan Transit Authority.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, THAT:

Section 1. That section A9-2, Levy of tax, of the Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Levy of tax.

The governing body of the City of Topeka, Kansas may levy a tax not to exceed 13 four and two-tenths (4.2) mills for 2015. For 2016 and subsequent years, the levy may 14 not  exceed  three  (3)  mills.  The  mill  levy  shall  be  upon  all  the  taxable  property  within 15 such city, as authorized by ordinance of the city, the proceeds of such tax levy to be 16 used by the metropolitan transit authority of Topeka, Kansas, pursuant to Article IX of 17 this code. 18

## STATEMENT OF MANNER OF ADOPTION OF FOREGOING

The foregoing Charter Ordinance No.  115 was passed on the 5th day of May, 2015, as shown by the minutes, Book 2015 Page 110, by a vote of 10 for and 0 against, being  not  less  than  two-thirds  (2/3)  of  the  members-elect  of  the  governing  body, published  in  the  Topeka  Metro  News,  the  official  city  newspaper,  on  the  11th  day  of May,  2015,  and  on  the  18th  day  of  May,  2015,  being  once  each  week  for  two  (2) consecutive weeks, and there being no petition demanding a referendum filed with the City Clerk within sixty (60) days after the final publication, said Charter Ordinance took effect on the 17th day of July, 2015.
