The City Attorney shall have the authority to initiate, file, and prosecute a civil lawsuit, in the name of and on behalf of the City, without the need of obtaining a resolution or ordinance authorizing said lawsuit from the Governing Body, so long as the initial damages claimed, based in tort or contract, by the City do not exceed $35,000 at the time of filing. (Ord. 20565 § 1, 6-10-25.)
