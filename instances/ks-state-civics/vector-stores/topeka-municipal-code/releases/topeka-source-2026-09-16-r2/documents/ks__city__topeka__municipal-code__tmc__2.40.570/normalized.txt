Amendments to the complaint may be permitted by the court before trial. Once the trial commences, the court may permit a complaint to be amended before judgment, if no additional or different offense is charged, and if substantial rights of the accused person are not prejudiced. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 60, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-95. Formerly 2.110.570.)
