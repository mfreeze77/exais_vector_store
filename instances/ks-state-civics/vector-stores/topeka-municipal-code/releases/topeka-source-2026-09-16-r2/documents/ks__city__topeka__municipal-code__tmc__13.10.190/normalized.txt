The water utility shall include fluoridation in the drinking water treatment operations, as determined by the Utilities Director, or his or her designee, and as recommended by the Kansas Department of Health and Environment.
Funds for equipment, operations and maintenance expenses to fluoridate the City’s water supply system shall be provided from the water, water pollution control, and stormwater utility fund. (Ord. 20089 § 11, 10-17-17.)
