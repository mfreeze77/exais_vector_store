## CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 97

A CHARTER ORDINANCE   introduced  by  City  Manager  Norton  N.  Bonaparte,  Jr. concerning  fire  department  promotions,  repealing  City  of Topeka Charter Ordinances No. A6-18.

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That City of Topeka Charter Ordinance, Topeka City Code Section A6-18, Positions filled by promotions, is hereby repealed:

## Positions filled by promotion.

All positions, other than third class fireman, shall be filled without further civil service 14 examination by promotion only after being interviewed by the promotion board, which board 15 shall  make  recommendations  of  qualified  personnel  to  the  chief,  who  shall  make 16 promotions from such recommendations. All promotions recommended by the promotion 17 board shall be on the basis of merit, seniority and personal record; provided however, the 18 chief of the department, with approval of the governing body, may, without 19 recommendations from said promotion board, make promotions of first assistant chief, 20 district  chief  battalion  chief,  fire  department  communication  officer,  fire  department 21 mechanic,  fire  marshal,  training  officer,  superintendent  of  buildings  or  other  specialty 22 departments that may be created. 23

## STATEMENT OF MANNER OF ADOPTION OF FOREGOING

The  foregoing  Charter  Ordinance  No.  97  was  passed  on  the  11th  day  of July, 2006, as shown by the minutes, Book 2006 Page 322, by a vote of 7 for and 3 against, being not less than 2/3 of the members-elect of the governing body, published in the Topeka Metro News, the official city newspaper, on the 19th day of July, 2006, and on the 26th day of  July, 2006, being once each week for two consecutive weeks, and there being no petition demanding a referendum filed with the City Clerk within sixty days after the final publication, said charter ordinance took effect on the 25th day of September, 2006.
