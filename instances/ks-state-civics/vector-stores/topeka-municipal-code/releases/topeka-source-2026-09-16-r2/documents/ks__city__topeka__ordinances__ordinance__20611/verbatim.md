## AN ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20611

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez annexing land to the City of Topeka, Kansas, in accordance with K.S.A. 12-520, halfway between SW Gage and SW Burlingame approximately ¼-mile north of SW 45 th  Street. (A25/6) (Council District No. 7)

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1.

That  the  following  described  land,  meeting  the  conditions  for  annexation prescribed in K.S.A. 12-520(a)(7), is hereby annexed and made a part of the City of Topeka, Kansas:

BEGINNING at the Northeast Corner of said Southwest Quarter; thence S01°27'35"E (State Plane Bearings) along the East line of said Southwest Quarter a distance of 1,359.49 feet to the Northeast corner of Misty Harbor Estates No. 5; thence S88°32'28"W along the North line of Misty Harbor Estates No. 5 a distance of 448.92 feet (449.05 feet platted); thence continuing along said North line on a non-tangent curve to the left, having a radius of 500.00 feet, an arc distance of

5.01 feet, and a long chord of S03°31'21"E a distance of 5.01 feet; thence continuing along said North line S88°31'32"W a distance  of  580.44  feet  (580.41  feet  platted)  to  the  Northwest  corner  of  Misty  Harbor Estates No. 5, said corner also being on the East line of Misty Harbor Estates No. 2; thence N01°28'47"W along the East line of Misty Harbor Estates No. 2 a distance of 129.98 feet; thence continuing along said East line N88°31'13"E a distance of 27.13 feet; thence continuing along said East line N01°27'51"W a distance  of  522.53  feet;  thence  continuing  along  said  East  line  N88°31'13"E  a  distance  of  75.00  feet; thence continuing along said East line N01°28'47"W a distance of 281.87 feet; thence continuing along said East line N70°59'43"W a distance of 57.83 feet; thence continuing along said East line S88°31'13"W a distance of 53.58 feet; thence continuing along said East line N10°39'01"E a distance of 250.08 feet; thence continuing along said East line on a non-tangent curve to the left, having a radius of 537.50 feet, an arc distance of 22.93 feet, and a long chord of N80°34'17"W a distance of 22.92 feet; thence continuing along said East line N08°12'24"E a distance of 150.09 feet (149.95 feet platted) to the Northeast corner of Misty Harbor Estates No. 2, said corner also being on the North line of said Southwest Quarter; thence N87°46'04"E  along  said  North  line  a  distance  of  979.92  feet  (979.95  feet  platted)  to  the  POINT  OF BEGINNING. The above described tract contains 30.76 acres (1,339,905 sq. ft.), more or less.

Section 2. That all land described in Section 1 of this Ordinance is taken into and made a part of the City for all City purposes and is assigned to City Council District No. 7.

Section 3.

This Ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its   passage, approval and publication in the official city newspaper.

Section 4. Upon passage and publication, the City Clerk shall file a certified copy   of this Ordinance with the County Clerk, the Register of Deeds, and the Shawnee County Election Commissioner.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, November 18, 2025.

Michael A. Padilla, Mayor
