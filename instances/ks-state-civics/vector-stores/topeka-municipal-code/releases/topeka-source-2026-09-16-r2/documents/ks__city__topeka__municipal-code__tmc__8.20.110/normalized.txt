(a) The purpose of this article is to provide for the adjudication of violations of provisions under this chapter regulating smoking in public places within the City and to establish a fair and efficient system for the enforcement of such provisions.
(b) The Police Chief or his or her designee shall be responsible for enforcing the provisions of this chapter within the City.
(c) Any person may register a complaint under this chapter to initiate enforcement with the Police Chief or his or her designee.
(d) Any law enforcement officer shall be authorized to issue a uniform complaint and notice to appear. (Ord. 19783 § 5, 12-18-12.)
