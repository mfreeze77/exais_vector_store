All bonds provided for in this chapter shall be signed and executed by the party from whom such bonds are required in addition to the sureties on such bonds, and except where otherwise provided by law the bonds shall be made to the City. All bonds provided for in this chapter shall be submitted to, and approved by, the City Attorney. (Ord. 17226 § 81, 1-27-98. Code 1995 § 2-307.)
