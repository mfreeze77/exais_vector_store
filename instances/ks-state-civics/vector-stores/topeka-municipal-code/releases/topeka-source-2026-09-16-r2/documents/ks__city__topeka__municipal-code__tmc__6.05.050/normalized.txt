Unless otherwise specifically provided herein, the judge of the Municipal Court of the City shall, upon a conviction of any section in this title, other than TMC 6.15.170 , sentence the owner, keeper, possessor or harborer of such animal as follows:
(a) Fine. A fine of not less than $1.00 or more than $499.00; or
(b) Imprisonment. Imprisonment in the City jail for not more than 179 days; or
(c) Both Fine and Imprisonment. A fine and imprisonment, both in accordance with subsections (a) and (b) of this section. (Ord. 20106 § 5, 12-19-17.)
