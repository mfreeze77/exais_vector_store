## BE  IT  ORDAINED  BY  THE  GOVERNING  BODY  OF  THE  CITY  OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, THAT:

Section 1. Nature of the Project.  The general nature of the proposed project (the  'CID  Project')  to  be  undertaken  within  the  District  is  as  follows:  hotel  and    site improvements, including, but not limited to, ADA accessibility, lighting, fire/safety,    HVAC systems, elevator repair, and general  maintenance.

Section 2. Findings.  The Governing Body finds the District furthers economic development within the City and further finds it advisable and in the City's best interest to establish  the  District  and  authorize  the  CID  Project  as  proposed,  as  described  in  the Topeka  Development  Corporation  Contract  No.  2025-01  (the  'Purchase  and    Sale Agreement for Hotel  Topeka').

Section  3. Estimated  Project   Cost. The estimated cost of the CID    Project within the District is approximately  $6,000,000.

Section 4. Legal Description.   The legal description of the     District is: Lot 3, Block A, Kansas Expocentre Subdivision No. 4 to the City of Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas (Assessor's Parcel/Tax Parcel ID number  133-06-0-30-04-001-020).

Section 5. Map.  A map of the District is attached as Exhibit A.

Section  6. Method of Financing; Sales Tax.  The method of financing the  CID Project is through a CID sales tax. The proposed amount of the CID sales tax to be levied is  two  percent  (2%)  which  shall be  levied  upon all  sales  of  tangible  personal  property  at retail or services taxable within the CID District pursuant to the Kansas Retailers'  Sales

## SUMMARY OF ORDINANCE NO.

On March 10, 2026, the City Council of the City of Topeka, Kansas (the 'City') passed an ordinance entitled:

AN  ORDINANCE  INTRODUCED  BY  CITY  MANAGER  DR.  ROBERT  M. PEREZ,  ESTABLISHING  A  COMMUNITY  IMPROVEMENT  DISTRICT (CID)  AT  17 TH  AND  TOPEKA  BOULEVARD  FOR  THE  HOTEL  TOPEKA PROPERTY,  AND  LEVYING  A  CID  SALES  TAX  OF  TWO    PERCENT (2.0%) WITHIN THE  CID.

The Ordinance approves community improvement district generally located southwest  of  the  intersection  of  S.W.  17 th Street  and  S.W.  Topeka  Boulevard.  The Ordinance  also  approves  the  imposition  of  2%  sales  tax  within  such  district  to  finance redevelopment costs within the district on a 'pay-as-you-go' basis. A complete text of the Ordinance may be obtained or viewed free of charge at the office of the City Clerk, City Hall,  215  S.E.  7th  Street,  1st  Floor,  Topeka,  Kansas  66603.  A  reproduction  of    the Ordinance  is  available  for  not  less  than  7  days  following  the  publication  date  of  this Summary  at  https://www.topeka.gov.

This Summary is hereby certified to be legally accurate and sufficient pursuant to the laws of the State of Kansas.

DATED: March 10, 2026.
