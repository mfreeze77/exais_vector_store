- 17 Section 2. That section 18.225.010, Special Use Requirements [(k) Dwelling Units on 18 Main Floor], of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as  follows:

- 19 Special Use Requirements.

- 20 (k) Dwelling Units on Main Floor. Dwelling units located on main floors shall meet the 21 following requirements:

- 22 (1) The units must be subordinate in area or location to nonresidential uses on 23 the main floor; or

- 24 (2) The units shall be allowed in structures that were originally built for use as 25 dwelling units, the structure has been used historically for dwelling units, or the  dwelling 26 units were converted from hospital, school, or hotel rooms.

- 27 (3) For land classified D-1 or X-3, requirements in paragraphs 1 and 2 of  this 28 section for Dwellings on Main Floor apply exclusively to parcels of land and buildings 29 abutting  Kansas  Avenue  between  6 th Avenue and 10 th   Avenue,  and  between  Gordon

- 30 Street and Norris Street.

- 31 (4) Conditional   Use   Permit. Land   use   development   not   meeting   the 32 requirements  of  this  section  may  be  approved  by  conditional  use  permit  pursuant  to 33 Chapter 18.215 TMC.
