The Code Official shall notify the owner of the cost incurred by the City to repair, vacate and close the structure, or demolish the structure. The notice shall state that the cost is due within 30 days of the date of the notice. If the cost is not paid within the 30-day time period, the City may impose a special assessment against the property on which the structure was located as authorized in K.S.A. 17-4759 and amendments thereto. (Ord. 20145 § 7, 10-2-18.)
