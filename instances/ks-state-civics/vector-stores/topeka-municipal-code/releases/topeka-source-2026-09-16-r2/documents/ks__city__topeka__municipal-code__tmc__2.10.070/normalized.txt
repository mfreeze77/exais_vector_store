Any person requesting access to an open public record for purposes of inspecting or copying such record, or obtaining a copy thereof, shall abide by the procedures adopted by the City Council for record inspection and copying, including those procedures established by the record custodian as authorized by the City Council. Such procedures shall be posted in each City office. (Ord. 20206 § 3, 9-10-19; Ord. 17226 § 18, 1-27-98. Code 1995 § 2-85. Formerly 2.20.070.)
