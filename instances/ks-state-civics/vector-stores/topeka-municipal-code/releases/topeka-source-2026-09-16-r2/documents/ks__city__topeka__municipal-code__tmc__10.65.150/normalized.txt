(a) As an alternative to all other rates of fare of taxicabs, a taxicab business may offer a share-a-ride program, the elements of which include the grouping of passengers and the cost per passenger determined on a per square mile basis. The share-a-ride program shall be optional to the passenger. The maximum rates of fare for conveying persons in the share-a-ride program shall be as follows:
(1) | For the first square mile | $3.00
(2) | For each succeeding square mile | $1.00
(3) | For each person age 12 or over riding with the passenger | $0.25

(b) The rate of fare to the passenger shall be determined as if the trip was a straight line from point of origin to point of destination; however, a taxicab driver may deviate from the straight line trip and pick up and deliver additional passengers. (Code 1981 § 46-42.2. Code 1995 § 154-122.)
