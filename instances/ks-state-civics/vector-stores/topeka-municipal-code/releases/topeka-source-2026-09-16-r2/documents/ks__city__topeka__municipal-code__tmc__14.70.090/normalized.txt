If in the moving of any building or structure any person shall cause damage to any street, alley, sidewalk or other public grounds or property belonging to the City, the damage shall be repaired under the direction and supervision of the City Engineer, and it shall be the duty of the City Engineer to inspect the streets, alleys, sidewalks and other public grounds over which the building or structure has been moved within two days after the moving thereof and to ascertain the damage, if any, done. (Code 1981 § 10-533. Code 1995 § 26-589.)
