The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this chapter, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this chapter, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning:
“Customer” or “consumer” means any person, for-profit or not-for-profit organization, firm, association or corporation, or political or taxing authority receiving City utility materials, equipment and/or services provided by the City’s water, wastewater and stormwater utility. (Ord. 19300 § 1, 8-25-09. Code 1995 § 146-9.)
