(a) It shall be unlawful for any person, except a public officer or employee in the discharge of duty or those specifically authorized by the Director or designee of the Shawnee County Department of Parks and Recreations, to loiter about or be found in any public park in the City after 11:00 p.m. and until 6:00 a.m. the following day.
(b) It shall be unlawful for any person, except a public officer or employee in the discharge of duty or those specifically authorized by the Friends of the Topeka Zoo (“FOTZ”), in accordance with the License and Management Agreement dated February 9, 2021, and amendments thereto, to loiter about or be found in the Topeka Zoo and Conservation Center after 11:00 p.m. and until 6:00 a.m. the following day. (Ord. 20290 § 2, 4-5-21.)
