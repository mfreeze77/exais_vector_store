Section R106.1.2, Manufacturer’s installation instructions, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:
Manufacturer’s installation instructions and specifications, as required by this code, shall be available on the job site at the time of the applicable inspection. This list shall include, but is not limited to, specification and installation sheets for:
1. Truss roof systems – specifications must include truss member sizing and all required permanent bracing locations for the system.
2. Engineered floor systems (each floor).
(Ord. 19727 § 11, 4-24-12.)
