The following words, terms, and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning:
“Law enforcement officer” means a sworn law enforcement officer employed by the City of Topeka Police Department.
“No-knock warrant” means any search warrant issued by a judge of the district court that does not require the law enforcement officer(s) executing the warrant to knock and announce themselves, and their purpose, prior to entering a specified premises.
“Search warrant” for purposes of this chapter means a legal document authorizing a law enforcement officer to enter and search a specified premises. (Ord. 20249 § 1, 7-21-20.)
