When any unplatted ground or platted lots upon which any special assessment has been levied by the City are sold in such a manner as to require the City Clerk to split the special assessments, all such special assessments on all such platted lots or on unplatted ground of the sum of $250.00 or less shall be paid in full not less than 30 days after such sale. (Code 1981 § 35-1. Code 1995 § 126-1.)
