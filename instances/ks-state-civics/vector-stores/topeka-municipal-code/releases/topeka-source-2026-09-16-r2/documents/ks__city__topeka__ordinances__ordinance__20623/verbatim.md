AN ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20623

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez relating to designation of street names, more specifically changing the name of NW 8 th  Street in Meier's Place Subdivision to NW Waldo Way. (SNC25/01) (Council District No. 2)

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. The official name of NW 8 th  Street within the City of Topeka, Kansas, that segment of NW 8 th  Street commencing from the point where NW 8 th  Street intersects with NW Vail Avenue and ending at a point approximately 975 feet east of NW Vail Avenue at NW Courtland Avenue in Meier's Place Subdivision, is hereby changed to NW Waldo Way. Provided that this ordinance shall not be construed to alter the dimensions of said street nor the setback requirements provided in other ordinances of the City of Topeka. Provided further, that this ordinance shall not be construed to affect the designation of said street as may be provided in other ordinances of the City of  Topeka.

Section 2. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage, approval and publication in the official city newspaper.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, Kansas on December 16, 2025.

Michael A. Padilla, Mayor

ATTEST:

Brenda Younger, City Clerk
