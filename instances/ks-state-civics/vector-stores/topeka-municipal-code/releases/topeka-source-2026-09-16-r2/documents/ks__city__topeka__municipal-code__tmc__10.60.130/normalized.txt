(a) It shall be unlawful for any person to stop, stand or park a vehicle:
(1) In any City-owned parking lot in which parking is reserved by the posting of signs authorized by the Transportation Operations Superintendent.
(2) In any on-street parking stall which has been reserved by the posting of signs authorized by the Transportation Operations Superintendent.
(b) Provided, however, those vehicles displaying the proper permit issued by the Transportation Operations Superintendent shall not be in violation of this section. (Ord. 17956 § 1, 2-4-03. Code 1995 § 142-356.)
