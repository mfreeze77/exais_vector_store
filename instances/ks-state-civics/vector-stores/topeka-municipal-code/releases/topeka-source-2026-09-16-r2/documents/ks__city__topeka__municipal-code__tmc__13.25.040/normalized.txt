The City shall adopt an operating budget for each fiscal year. The operating budget shall set forth for such fiscal year the estimated revenues and the estimated costs for operations and maintenance, extension and replacement and debt service. (Ord. 19496 § 120, 12-21-10; Ord. 17059 § 13, 12-17-96. Code 1995 § 146-380. Formerly 14.85.050.)
