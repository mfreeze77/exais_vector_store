It shall be the duty and responsibility of the Chief of Police and the Chief of the Fire Department to cause to be made, kept and maintained a separate payroll record on each member of the department appointed under civil service. Payroll records will be kept according to the provisions of the personnel code. (Ord. 20206 § 28, 9-10-19; Ord. 16514 § 31(31-354), 9-15-92. Code 1995 § 106-131. Formerly 2.125.370.)
