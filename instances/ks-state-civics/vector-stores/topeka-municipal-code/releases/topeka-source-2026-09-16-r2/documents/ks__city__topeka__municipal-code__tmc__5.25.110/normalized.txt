An accepted franchise confers on each grantee for the term thereof, not to exceed 20 years from the effective date of such award (or until terminated or surrendered prior to expiration of such term), the nonexclusive right to provide ambulance service in the City in accordance with this chapter and to use the City streets and alleys for operation of such ambulance service. The franchise does not confer rights other than as provided by this chapter and the franchise granted. (Code 1981 § 6-25. Code 1995 § 62-48.)
