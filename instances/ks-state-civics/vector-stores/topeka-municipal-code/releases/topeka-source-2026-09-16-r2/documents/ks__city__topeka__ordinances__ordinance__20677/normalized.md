NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that:

Section 1. The above recitals are incorporated herein by reference as if set forth in full.

Section 2. The Governing Body hereby establishes and imposes, within the City limits,  a  twelve-month  temporary  moratorium  on  the  acceptance,  processing,  and approval of data center applications. No City official, board, or body shall accept, process, review, approve, or issue any zoning permits, building permits, site plan approvals, or utility connections requests for the establishment or expansion of any data center located within or outside of the City limits from the effective date of this ordinance until either twelve  months  from  the  effective  date  of  this  ordinance  or  until  the  Governing  Body amends or repeals this ordinance, whichever occurs earlier.

Section 3. This moratorium shall not apply to any data center applications that have received final formal approval prior to the effective date of this ordinance, nor to routine maintenance, equipment upgrades or improvements of existing permitted data centers that do not increase the footprint or utility load of the facility.

Section 4. Ordinance No. 20670 is hereby repealed.

Section 5. If any section, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is found to be unconstitutional or is otherwise held invalid by any court of competent jurisdiction, it
