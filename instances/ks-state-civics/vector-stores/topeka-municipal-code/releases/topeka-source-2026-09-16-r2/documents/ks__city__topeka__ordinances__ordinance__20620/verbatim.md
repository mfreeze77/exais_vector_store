## ORDINANCE NO. 20620

- AN ORDINANCE introduced  by  City  Manager  Dr.  Robert  M.  Perez,  establishing  a service  fee  levy  for  2026  for  the  NOTO  Business Improvement District,  amending  §  3.70.030  of  the  Topeka  Municipal  Code  and repealing original section.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1.

That  section  3.70.030,  Method  of  financing  -  Service  fee,  of  The

- 12 Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:
- 13 Method of financing - Service fee.
- 14 (a) The  district  will  be  financed  by  imposition  of  an  annual  service  fee for 15 20252026 on businesses within the following tier levels:

- Section 2. That original § 3.70.030 of The Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby specifically repealed.
