Every person charged with violation of an ordinance shall receive a copy of the complaint, and shall not be required to plead until he or she shall have had a reasonable time to examine the same, to obtain counsel and to determine his or her plea. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 37, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-72. Formerly 2.110.340.)
