If it appears based upon a preponderance of the evidence that one or both of the partners in a registered domestic partnership no longer meets the definition of a domestic partner under this chapter, the City shall, after notice and an opportunity to be heard, remove the partnership from the registry. The City Manager shall develop procedures for the implementation of this section. (Ord. 20206 § 14, 9-10-19; Ord. 19905 § 6, 5-20-14. Formerly 2.150.060.)
