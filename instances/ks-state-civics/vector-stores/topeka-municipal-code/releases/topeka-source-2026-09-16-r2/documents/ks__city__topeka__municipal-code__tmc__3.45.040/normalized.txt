It shall be the duty of the City Clerk to cause a notice of hearing to be mailed, postage prepaid, to each person owning property or responsible for payment of taxes within the proposed special benefit district as determined from the tax rolls of the Treasurer of the County, and to each occupant residing in the benefit district. Such notice shall be mailed at least 30 days prior to the date of such public hearing. (Code 1981 § 35-20. Code 1995 § 126-38.)
