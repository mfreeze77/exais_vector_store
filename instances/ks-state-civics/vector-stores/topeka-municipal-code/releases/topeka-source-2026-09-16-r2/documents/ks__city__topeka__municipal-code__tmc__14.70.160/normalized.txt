When, in the opinion of the City Engineer or the City Forester, the building or structure to be moved cannot be moved without serious irreparable damage or destruction, then they shall withhold their approval and so note “disapproved” on the moving permit. (Ord. 16513 § 11(10-589), 9-15-92. Code 1995 § 26-608.)
