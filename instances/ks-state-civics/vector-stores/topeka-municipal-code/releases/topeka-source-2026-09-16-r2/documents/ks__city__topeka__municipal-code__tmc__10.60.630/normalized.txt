(a) It shall be unlawful for any person to move or attempt to move any vehicle that has an immobilization device attached thereto, except as authorized by a police officer or a parking control officer.
(b) It shall be unlawful for any person to tamper with an immobilization device or remove or attempt to remove an immobilization device placed thereon by a parking control officer or police officer.
(c) Any person violating this section may be punished by:
(1) A fine of not more than $499.00;
(2) Imprisonment in jail for not more than 179 days; or
(3) Both such fine and imprisonment not to exceed the limits set out in subsection (c)(1) and (c)(2) of this section. (Ord. 19589 § 3, 6-14-11.)
