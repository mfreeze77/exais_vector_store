The Council shall with respect to all actions pursuant to this chapter give due consideration to the ambulance service resolutions of the Board of County Commissioners and to any contract awarded and accepted thereunder. A recommendation may be requested from any County-City advisory council, committee, task force, or group created to provide subject matter expertise regarding the emergency medical services system when any question regarding cooperation between the City and the County exists or is foreseen. (Ord. 20557 § 3, 4-8-25.)
