It shall be unlawful and an offense for any person or driver to deposit or cause to be deposited in any parking meter any slug, device or substitute for a coin of the United States otherwise required to be deposited in a parking meter, or to deposit a bent or damaged coin. (Ord. 17707 § 5, 7-31-01; Ord. 17635 § 5, 1-9-01. Code 1981 § 43-439. Code 1995 § 142-380.)
