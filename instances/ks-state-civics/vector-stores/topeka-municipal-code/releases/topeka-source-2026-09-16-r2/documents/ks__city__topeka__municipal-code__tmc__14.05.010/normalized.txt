It shall be unlawful for any person engaged in or having charge of the construction of any building or any public or private improvement in the City to fail, neglect or refuse to provide a good, suitable and sanitary water closet, on the premises where the building is located or within a convenient distance of such improvement, for the use of the workers employed in the construction of such building or improvement. (Code 1981 § 10-2. Code 1995 § 26-1.)
