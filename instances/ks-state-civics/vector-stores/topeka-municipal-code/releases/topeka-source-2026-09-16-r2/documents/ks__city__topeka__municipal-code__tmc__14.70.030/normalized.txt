No building or structure shall be moved pursuant to this chapter without notice to the Chief of the Fire Department and the Chief of the Police Department given by the permittee under this chapter. Upon receipt of such notice, the Chief of the Police Department shall provide the permittee a schedule setting forth the hours the building or structure shall be moved and further shall provide a police escort which shall accompany the building or structure as it is being moved. (Ord. 16513 § 3(10-527), 9-15-92. Code 1995 § 26-583.)
