## ORDINANCE NO. 20676

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez amending the 'District Map' referred to and made a part of the Zoning Ordinances by Section 18.50.050 of the Topeka Municipal Code, by providing for certain changes in zoning on 10 acres of property located at 4212 and 4236 SW Burlingame Road FROM 'R-1" Single-Family Dwelling District TO 'I-1' Light Industrial District. (Z26/06) (Council District No. 5)
