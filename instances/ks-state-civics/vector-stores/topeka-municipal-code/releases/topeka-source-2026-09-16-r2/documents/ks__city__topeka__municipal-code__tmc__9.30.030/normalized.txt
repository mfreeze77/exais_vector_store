It shall be unlawful for any person to post, paste, paint, brand or stamp any printed, written, painted or other advertisement, bill, poster, dodger, bulletin or sign or notice of any kind or character upon any private fence, house or other building, or upon any other private property in the corporate limits without consent of the owner thereof. (Ord. 20299 § 72, 6-1-21. Code 1981 § 3-2. Code 1995 § 6-2. Formerly 9.10.020.)
