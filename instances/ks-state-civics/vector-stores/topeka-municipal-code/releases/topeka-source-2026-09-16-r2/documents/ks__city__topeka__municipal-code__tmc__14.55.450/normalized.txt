Section R403.1.2, Continuous footing in Seismic Design Categories D 0 , D 1 and D 2 , is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provisions shall be substituted therefor:
Exterior walls of buildings shall be supported by continuous footings. Other footing materials or systems shall be designed in accordance with accepted engineering practice.
(Ord. 20467 § 23, 12-5-23.)
