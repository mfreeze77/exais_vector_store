Every pawnbroker shall, in the absolute purchase of any personal property, enter such purchase in the register in the same manner as property received on pledge, and shall, for 15 calendar days after such purchase, keep such property at the place of business subject to inspection by officers as fully and to the same extent as goods received on pledge. (Ord. 16376 § 2(38-59), 10-1-91. Code 1995 § 114-95.)
