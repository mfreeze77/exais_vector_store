(a) There is hereby created a parkland acquisition and development fund. The purpose of the fund shall be to act as a depository for moneys to be used for the purchase and development of land for park purposes pursuant to TMC 18.40.130 .
(b) The County shall administer the acquisition of parkland and parkland development fees pursuant to TMC 18.40.130 . Fees collected by the City shall be transmitted to the County within 30 days of receipt. (Ord. 19715 § 1, 3-20-12.)
