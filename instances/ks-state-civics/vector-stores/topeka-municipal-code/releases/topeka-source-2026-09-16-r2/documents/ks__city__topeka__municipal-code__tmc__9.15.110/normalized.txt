It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a private room in a premises licensed to sell cereal malt beverages; provided, that this section shall not apply if the licensed premises are also currently licensed by the State Director of Alcoholic Beverage Control. (Code 1981 § 5-21. Code 1995 § 10-52.)
