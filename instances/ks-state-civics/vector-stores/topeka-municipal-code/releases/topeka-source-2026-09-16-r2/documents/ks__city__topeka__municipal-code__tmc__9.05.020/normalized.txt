The doing of any of the acts or things defined, made unlawful or a misdemeanor, or the failing to do any of the things commanded to be done, as specified and set forth in this title, in the City, is hereby declared to be an offense against the good order, public peace, morals, health, proper government and welfare of the City and unlawful. Any person found guilty of violating any of the provisions of this title shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and punished as provided in TMC 1.10.070 . (Code 1981 § 15-1. Code 1995 § 54-2.)
