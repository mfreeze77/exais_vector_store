A floodplain development permit shall be required for all proposed construction or other development, including the placement of manufactured homes or mobile homes, in the areas described in TMC 17.30.070 . No person, firm, corporation, or unit of government shall initiate any development or substantial improvement or cause the same to be done without first obtaining a separate floodplain development permit for each structure or other development. (Ord. 20425 § 7, 6-20-23.)
