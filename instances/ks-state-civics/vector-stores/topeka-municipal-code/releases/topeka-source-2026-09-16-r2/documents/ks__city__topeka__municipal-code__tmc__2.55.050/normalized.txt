It is the intent of this article to provide assistance in any form of service including, but not limited to, police, fire, EMS, public works, administrative and clerical during times of disaster as defined in K.S.A. 12-16,117 ( 1994 Supp.) with all the privileges and immunities described therein. (Ord. 20206 § 7, 9-10-19; Ord. 16866 § 3, 9-26-95. Code 1995 § 38-113. Formerly 2.100.050.)
