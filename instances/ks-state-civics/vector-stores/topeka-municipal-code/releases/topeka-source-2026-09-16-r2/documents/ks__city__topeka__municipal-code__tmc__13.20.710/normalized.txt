The Utilities Director shall have the authority to accept liquid waste for treatment at the plant and to establish the rate for treatment of any class of liquid waste. The Utilities Director shall take certain factors into consideration in setting the rates for treatment of liquid waste including, but not limited to, surcharge rate, documented waste strength, special treatment requirements, special handling and administrative costs. Nothing contained herein shall limit the Utilities Director’s ability to refuse to accept any liquid waste for treatment. (Ord. 20089 § 90, 10-17-17.)
