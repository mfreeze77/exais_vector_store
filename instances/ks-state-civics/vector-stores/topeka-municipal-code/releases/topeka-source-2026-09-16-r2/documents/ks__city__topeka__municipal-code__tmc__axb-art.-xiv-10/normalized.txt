Upon termination of this Franchise, whether by lapse of time, by agreement between the Company and the City, or by forfeiture thereof, the Company shall have the right to remove any and all of its facilities and equipment used in its business within a reasonable time after such expiration, but in such event, it shall be the duty of the Company, immediately upon and during such removal; (a) to cooperate with the City and its agents to permit the installation of all necessary facilities and other equipment necessary or convenient for the provision of electric energy to residents of the City; and (b) to restore the streets, avenues, alleys, parks, and other public ways and grounds from which said facilities, and other equipment have been removed, to the equivalent condition as the same were before said removal was effected. (Ord. 19856 § 10, 10-1-13.)
