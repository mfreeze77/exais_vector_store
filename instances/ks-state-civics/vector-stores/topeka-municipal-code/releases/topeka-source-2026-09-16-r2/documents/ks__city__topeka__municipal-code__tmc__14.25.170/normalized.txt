Section 507.3, Flood hazard areas, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provision shall be substituted therefor:
In flood hazard areas, repairs that constitute substantial improvement shall require that the building comply with the requirements provided in the TMC Chapters 14.20 (building code) and 17.30 (floodplain management).
(Ord. 20344 § 17, 2-15-22.)
