(a) Where a request has been made for inspection of any open public record which is readily available to the record custodian, there will be no inspection fee charged to the requester.
(b) In all cases not covered by subsection (a) of this section, a record inspection fee may be charged at a per hour rate determined by the records custodian comparatively based on the hourly rate of the employee engaged in the record search and production. (Ord. 20206 § 3, 9-10-19. Formerly 2.20.110.)
