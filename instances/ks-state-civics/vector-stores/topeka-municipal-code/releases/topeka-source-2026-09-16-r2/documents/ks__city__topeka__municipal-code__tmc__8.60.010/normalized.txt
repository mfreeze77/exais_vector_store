The International Property Maintenance Code and Commentary, including the guideline standards in Appendix A and excluding Appendix B, 2024 Edition, as published by the International Code Council and amended by this chapter (IPMC), is hereby incorporated by reference as the property maintenance code of the City of Topeka to address the public health, safety, and welfare relative to the use and maintenance of existing structures and premises save and except such articles, sections, parts or portions as are hereafter omitted, deleted, modified or changed. (Ord. 20625 § 1, 12-16-25.)
