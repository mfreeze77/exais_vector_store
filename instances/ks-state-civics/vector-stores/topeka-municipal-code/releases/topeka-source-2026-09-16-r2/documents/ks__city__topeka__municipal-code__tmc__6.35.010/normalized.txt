It shall be unlawful for any person, with no requirement of a culpable mental state, to keep livestock inside the City limits if in a building or lot that is located within 200 feet of his or her property line. Such building or lot shall comply with all applicable building, property maintenance, planning, zoning, and utility code provisions, in addition to any health and sanitation requirements that may be required by State or local law. (Ord. 20106 § 82, 12-19-17.)
