It shall be unlawful for any wholesaler or distributor to sell or deliver any cereal malt beverages to any licensee within the City:
(a) Before 6:00 a.m. or after 12:00 midnight Monday through Saturday;
(b) Before 12:00 noon or after 8:00 p.m. on Sunday;
(c) On any election day during the hours the polls are open; or
(d) On Easter, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day or Christmas Day. (Ord. 18642 § 1, 5-16-06; Ord. 18523 § 1, 10-11-05. Code 1981 § 5-38(b). Code 1995 § 10-37.)
