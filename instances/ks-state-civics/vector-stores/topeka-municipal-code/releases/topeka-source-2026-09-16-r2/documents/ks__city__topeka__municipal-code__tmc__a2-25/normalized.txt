(a) The Council and Mayor shall be the governing body of the city. It shall exercise the corporate powers of the city and, subject to the expressed limitations of this Charter Ordinance, it shall be vested with all powers of legislation in municipal affairs adequate to provide a complete system of local government consistent with the Constitution of the State of Kansas. It shall have no administrative powers.
(b) This section A2-25 of the charter ordinances of the City of Topeka shall only be amended or repealed upon approval by a majority of the electors voting during an election called for such purpose. (C.O. 96 § 3, 5-23-06/8-28-06; C.O. 94 § 11, 7-20-04.)
