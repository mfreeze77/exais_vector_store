Any such revenue bonds shall be issued as provided in K.S.A. 10-1201 et seq. except to the extent that such statutes are in conflict with this act; provided, before any revenue bonds shall be authorized or issued under the provisions of this act the public building commission shall adopt a resolution specifying the amount of such bonds, the purpose of the issuance thereof and stating that if within 30 days after the last date of publication of such notice a petition in opposition to the same, signed by not less than seven percent of the electors of such county within which the city is located, is filed with the county clerk, the board shall submit the question to the voters at an election called for such purpose or at the next general election. Such resolution shall be published once a week for three consecutive weeks in a newspaper having general circulation in such county. (C.O. 55 § 2, 3-6-82.)
