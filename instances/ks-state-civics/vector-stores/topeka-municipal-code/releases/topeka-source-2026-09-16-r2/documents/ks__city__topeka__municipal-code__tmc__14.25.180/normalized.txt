Section 701.3, Flood hazard areas, is hereby deleted in its entirety and the following provision shall be substituted therefor:
In flood hazard areas, alterations that constitute substantial improvement shall require that the building comply with TMC Chapters 14.20 (building code), 14.55 (residential code), as applicable, and 17.30 (floodplain management).
(Ord. 20344 § 18, 2-15-22.)
