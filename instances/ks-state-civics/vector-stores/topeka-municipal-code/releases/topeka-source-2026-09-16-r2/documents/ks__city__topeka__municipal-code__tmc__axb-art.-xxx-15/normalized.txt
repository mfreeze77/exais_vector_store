In the event Grantee ceases to operate and abandons Grantee’s Network and Facilities or any part thereof, for a period of ninety (90) days or more, Grantee shall, at its sole cost and expense, vacate and remove the abandoned Facility within one hundred and eighty (180) days. Grantee shall also, at its sole cost and expense, restore the PROW to its original condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Alternatively, the City may allow Grantee, in the City’s sole and absolute discretion, to abandon Grantee’s Facilities, or any part thereof, in place and convey it to the City. (Ord. 20044 § 15, 12-20-16.)
