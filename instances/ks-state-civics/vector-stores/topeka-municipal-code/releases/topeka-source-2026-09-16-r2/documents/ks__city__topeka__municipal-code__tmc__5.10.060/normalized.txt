(a) The license fee for auctioneers shall be as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule.
(b) Expert nonresident pedigreed livestock auctioneers when crying sales in connection with local licensed auctioneers, at which pedigreed livestock is sold, shall not be required to pay a license fee. (Ord. 20631 § 9, 1-13-26.)
State Law References: Limitations on license fee for certain auctioneers, K.S.A. 58-1019 , 58-1023 .
