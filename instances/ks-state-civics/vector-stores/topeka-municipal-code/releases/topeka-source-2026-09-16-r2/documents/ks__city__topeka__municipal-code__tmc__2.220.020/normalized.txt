The Board of Zoning Appeals shall consist of seven members appointed by the Mayor. None of the members shall hold any other public office by the City except that two members may be members of the Topeka Planning Commission. The appointees shall reside inside the corporate area of the City of Topeka. (Ord. 20206 § 78, 9-10-19; Ord. 18288 § 3, 7-13-04; Ord. 17156 § 7, 8-12-97. Code 1995 § 48-34.01. Formerly 2.45.020.)
