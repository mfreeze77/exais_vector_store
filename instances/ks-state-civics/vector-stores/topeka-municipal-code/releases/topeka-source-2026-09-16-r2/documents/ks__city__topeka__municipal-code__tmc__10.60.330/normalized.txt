No person shall be parked in a parking space restricted by time limitations beyond the specified time period. The time period shall:
(a) Begin when the vehicle is first parked in a parking space restricted by time limitations;
(b) Continue whether or not the vehicle is thereafter moved to another parking space restricted by time limitations within the central business district during the same calendar date; and
(c) Include all time during which the vehicle is thereafter parked in another single vehicle parking place having the same maximum lawful time limit unless movement of the vehicle has terminated the period as provided in subsection (b) of this section. (Ord. 20566 § 3, 6-10-25.)
