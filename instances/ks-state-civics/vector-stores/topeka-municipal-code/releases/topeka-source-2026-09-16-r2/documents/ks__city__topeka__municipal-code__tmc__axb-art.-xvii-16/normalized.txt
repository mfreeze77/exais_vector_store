Should IdeaTek after the Parties’ execution and delivery of this Agreement enter into a franchise agreement with another municipality of the same size or smaller than the City in this State, which agreement contains financial benefits for such municipality which, taken as a whole and balanced with the other terms of such agreement, are in the City’s opinion substantially superior to those in this Franchise Agreement, the City shall have the right to require that IdeaTek modify this Franchise Agreement to incorporate the same or substantially similar superior benefits. (Ord. 20581 § 16, 8-12-25.)
