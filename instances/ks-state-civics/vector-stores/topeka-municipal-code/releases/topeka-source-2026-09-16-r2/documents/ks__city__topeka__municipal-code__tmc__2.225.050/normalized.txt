(a) The Topeka Planning Commission shall convene for its first meeting at such time and place as shall be fixed by the City Manager of the City, and shall thereupon proceed to organize and elect officers and fix and determine times and places of future meetings, which meetings shall not be less frequent than four times a year.
(b) Special meetings of the Topeka Planning Commission may be called by the chairperson, or, if absent, by the vice-chairperson, on not less than three days’ notice, such notice to be by mail at the address given to the secretary of the Topeka Planning Commission by each member.
(c) The Topeka Planning Commission shall adopt bylaws to implement the provisions of this chapter in accordance with State law, a certified copy to be filed with the City Council.
