If the applicant for a taxicab driver’s license is denied the license, the applicant may appeal such decision to the district court of the County by:
(a) Filing a notice of appeal within 10 days after such denial with the clerk of the district court; and
(b) Filing a copy of such notice with the City Clerk. (Code 1981 § 46-82.1. Code 1995 § 154-106.)
