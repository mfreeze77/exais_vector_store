(a) Created – Director. There is hereby created a Department of Human Resources. The Director of Human Resources shall be appointed by the City Manager. The City Manager, pursuant to Charter Ordinance No. 94, shall be responsible for supervising and evaluating the performance of the Human Resources Director. The Human Resources Director shall be responsible for the overall operation of the Department and management of all personnel matters. (Ord. 20507 § 2, 8-13-24.)
Cross References: Personnel, Chapters 2.105 , 2.110 , 2.120 , 2.125 and 2.130 TMC.
The following sections refer to Human Resources Department responsibilities: law enforcement, TMC 2.35.010 ; departments, established positions, and employees, TMC 2.110.010 ; workers compensation claims, TMC 2.125.110 ; self-insurance fund, TMC 3.25.060 .
