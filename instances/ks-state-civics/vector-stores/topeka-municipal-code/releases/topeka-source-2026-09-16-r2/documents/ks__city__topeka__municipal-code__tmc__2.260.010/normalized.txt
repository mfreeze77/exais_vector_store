The Governing Body of the City, by the power vested in it by K.S.A. 12-5901 et seq., hereby establishes the City of Topeka Land Bank. The Land Bank shall serve the public purpose of providing for the orderly, planned and reutilization of abandoned, tax-foreclosed or otherwise underutilized properties.
Pursuant to K.S.A. 12-5902(d) , the Bank will be dissolved on December 31, 2029; or by ordinance prior to December 31, 2029. (Ord. 20657 § 1, 5-19-26.)
