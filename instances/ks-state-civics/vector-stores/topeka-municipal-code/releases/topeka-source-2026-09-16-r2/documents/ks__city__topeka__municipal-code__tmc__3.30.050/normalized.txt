The Contracts and Procurement Director or designee shall administer this chapter and may promulgate and amend rules and regulations, subject to approval by the Director of Administrative and Financial Services or designee, relating to any matter pertaining to the procurement of goods and services. (Ord. 20404 §§ 5, 6, 1-17-23.)
