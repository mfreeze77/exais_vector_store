Upon approval of this Charter Ordinance by majority vote of the electors of Topeka, Kansas, voting thereon, the Mayor and Council holding office at the time of such election shall continue in office as an interim governing body, holding all powers and duties as specified in the Kansas Constitution, Kansas statutes, and the Charter Ordinances and ordinary ordinances of Topeka, Kansas, until the commencement of the term of office of the newly elected Council. (C.O. 94 § 25, 7-20-04.)
