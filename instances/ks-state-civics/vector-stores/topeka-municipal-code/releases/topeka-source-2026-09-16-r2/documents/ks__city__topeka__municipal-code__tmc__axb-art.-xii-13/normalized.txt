EMS shall hold the City harmless for all liability, damages, costs and expenses of every kind for the payment of which the City may become liable to any person by reason of the rights and privileges herein granted and, if any action either at law or in equity be brought against the City for damages or for any cost to the City for any fault of EMS, its servants, agents, or employees, in the operation of its ambulance service, EMS shall pay all costs, damages and expenses including costs of defense for which the City may be held liable.
