The members first appointed shall serve respectively for terms of one, two or three years, divided equally or as nearly equally as possible between the members. Thereafter, members shall be appointed for terms of three years each. Vacancies shall be filled by appointment for the unexpired term. The members shall serve without compensation. (Ord. 20206 § 78, 9-10-19; Ord. 18288 § 4, 7-13-04. Code 1995 § 48-34.02. Formerly 2.45.030.)
