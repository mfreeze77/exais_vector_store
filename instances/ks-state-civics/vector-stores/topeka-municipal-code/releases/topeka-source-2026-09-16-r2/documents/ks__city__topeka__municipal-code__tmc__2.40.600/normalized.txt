When a judgment is rendered, the Municipal Judge or clerk of the Municipal Court shall enter such judgment on the docket; however, the omission of this duty shall not affect the validity of the judgment. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 63, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-98. Formerly 2.110.600.)
