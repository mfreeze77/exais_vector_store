- Section 1. That  section  A2-29,  Vacancies  -  Forfeiture  of  office  -  Filling  of 11
- vacancies, of the Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as 12
- 13

follows:

- Vacancies - Forfeiture of office - Filling of vacancies. 14
- (a) 15
- Vacancies.  The  office  of  district  Councilmember  or  Mayor  shall  become
- vacant upon death, resignation, recall, removal from office in any manner authorized by 16
- law, or by forfeiture of office pursuant to K.S.A. 60-1205, as amended. 17
- (b) Filling of vacancies.  A  vacancy  in  the offices of Mayor  or  district 18 Councilmember shall be filled by appointment of an elector qualified to fill the vacant 19 position  by  a  majority  vote  of  the  remaining  members  of  the  governing  body.  If  the 20 vacancy occurs before June 1 in an election year, the unexpired term shall be filled at 21 the next primary and general election in the same manner as if the term were expiring 22
- and  the  term  of  the  appointed  member  shall  end  on  the  second  Monday  in  January 23
- following certification of the election. 24

## CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 119

A CHARTER ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Brent Trout, amending City of Topeka Charter Ordinance No. 117, § 6, as codified at Section A2-29 of the Topeka Municipal Code.
