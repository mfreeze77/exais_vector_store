The maintenance and operation of an ambulance service by EMS in the City shall be in accordance with prevailing standards of care in the ambulance industry. All EMS personnel and ambulances shall be fully licensed or certified as required by law. EMS shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and City laws, rules, regulations, codes, and other requirements in connection with the operation of the ambulance service. (Ord. 20561 § 3, 5-20-25.)
