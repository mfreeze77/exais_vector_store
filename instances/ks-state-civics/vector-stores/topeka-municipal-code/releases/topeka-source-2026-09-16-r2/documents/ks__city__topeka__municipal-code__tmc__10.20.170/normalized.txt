(a) Every person, upon a conviction of a moving violation, as defined in K.S.A. 8-249 and Kansas Administrative Regulations 92-52-9 , in a school zone, shall be fined three times the amount on the fine schedule as set by the Municipal Court in accordance with TMC 2.40.330 , with the exception of those offenses excluded by TMC 2.40.330 ; such fine shall not be paroled or otherwise reduced.
(b) The Municipal Court is hereby authorized to establish mandatory court appearances for any person charged with violating a school zone speed limit in an amount the court finds to be excessive. (Ord. 19652 § 3, 10-11-11.)
