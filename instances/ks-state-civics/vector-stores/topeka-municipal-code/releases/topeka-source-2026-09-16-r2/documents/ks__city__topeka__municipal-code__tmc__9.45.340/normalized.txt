Camping on or under public infrastructure such as bus shelters, bridges, overpasses and flood control works endangers such infrastructure because of fires, obstructions and the accumulation of personal property. Camping in areas that include retail businesses, restaurants, hotels, private residences and event spaces developed to attract residents and tourists adversely affects such areas and impedes economic development. Accordingly, the purpose of this article is to protect public infrastructure and designated areas by regulating camping and the storage of personal property. (Ord. 20224 § 1, 12-3-19.)
