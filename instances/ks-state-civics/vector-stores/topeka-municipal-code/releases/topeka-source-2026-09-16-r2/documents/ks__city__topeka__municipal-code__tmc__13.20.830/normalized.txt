Each grease generator shall submit a quarterly grease report on the electronic form available on the City of Topeka utilities web page. The quarterly grease report shall be submitted on or before the last calendar day of March, June, September, and December in each year.
Each quarterly grease report shall provide the following information, including, but not limited to, a record of the dates the grease trap and/or grease interceptor was cleaned, the name and contact information for the grease hauler, if applicable, and grease generator contact information. Failure to submit a quarterly grease report or failure to submit a complete report within 30 days of the due date may result in the issuance of a notice of violation and enforcement under Article II of Chapter 13.15 TMC. (Ord. 20157 § 11, 12-4-18.)
