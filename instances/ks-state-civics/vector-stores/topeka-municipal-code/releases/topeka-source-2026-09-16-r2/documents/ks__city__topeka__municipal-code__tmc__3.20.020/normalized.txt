When an emergency exists so as to preclude delay of approval or disapproval of an application for the purpose of referring to the Director of Administrative and Financial Services and the Legal Department, the City Manager may authorize a grant without so referring such application. (Ord. 18383 § 11, 1-25-05; Ord. 17226 § 93, 1-27-98. Code 1995 § 2-347.)
