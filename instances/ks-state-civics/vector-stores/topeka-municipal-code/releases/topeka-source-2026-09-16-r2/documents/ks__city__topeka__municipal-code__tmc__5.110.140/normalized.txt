(a) No person shall engage or continue in business as a pawnbroker without first obtaining a license therefor as provided in this article.
(b) Not more than one place of business shall be maintained under the same license, but more than one license may be issued to the same licensee upon compliance with all the provisions of this article governing the issuance of an initial license. (Code 1981 § 38-73. Code 1995 § 114-121.)
State Law References: Municipal license required, K.S.A. 16-707 ; licensing of multiple locations, K.S.A. 16-710 .
