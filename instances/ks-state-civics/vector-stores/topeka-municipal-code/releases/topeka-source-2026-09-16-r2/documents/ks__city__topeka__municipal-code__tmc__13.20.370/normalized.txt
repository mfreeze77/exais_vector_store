No user shall ever increase the use of process water or in any way attempt to dilute a discharge as a partial or complete substitute for adequate treatment in order to achieve compliance with discharge limitation unless expressly authorized by an applicable pretreatment standard or requirement. The Utilities Director may impose mass limitations on users who are using dilution to meet applicable pretreatment standards or requirements or in other cases when the imposition of mass limitations is warranted. (Ord. 20089 § 60, 10-17-17.)
