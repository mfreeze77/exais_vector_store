The document or other instrument evidencing the license of a pawnbroker shall state the address at which the business is to be conducted and shall state fully the name of the licensee, and if the licensee is a partnership, the names of members thereof, and if a corporation, the date and place of its incorporation and the names of all shareholders thereof. (Code 1981 § 38-78. Code 1995 § 114-126.)
State Law References: Similar provisions, K.S.A. 16-710 .
