Any judicial action or proceeding to attack, review, set aside or annul the reasonableness, legality, or validity of any impact fee must be filed and service of process effected within 30 days following the date of imposition of the fee or the final determination of the City Council, whichever is later. (Ord. 18273 § 13, 6-22-04. Code 1995 § 130-772.)
