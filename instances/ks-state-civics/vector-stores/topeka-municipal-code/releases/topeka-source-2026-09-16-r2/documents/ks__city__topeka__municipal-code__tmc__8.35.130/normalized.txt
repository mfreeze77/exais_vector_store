A complaint issued pursuant to the provisions of TMC 8.35.110 shall be in writing and shall be signed by the complainant. More than one violation may be charged in the same complaint. The complaint shall state the offense or violation charged and the corresponding ordinance or code authority. A complaint and notice to appear may be made in the form of a uniform complaint and notice to appear. A complaint shall be deemed sufficient if in substantially the form of a uniform complaint and notice to appear. (Ord. 17199 § 1, 10-28-97. Code 1981 § 16-151. Code 1995 § 70-73.)
