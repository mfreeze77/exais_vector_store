The following are hereby established as the monthly retail water service rates, effective on the dates specified, which the consumer shall pay for water services:
(a) Base Charge. A customer who has a City service line connected to the City water main shall pay a monthly base charge that is based upon the size of the water meter. This charge shall apply regardless of whether water has passed from the City water main through the City service line during the billing period.
