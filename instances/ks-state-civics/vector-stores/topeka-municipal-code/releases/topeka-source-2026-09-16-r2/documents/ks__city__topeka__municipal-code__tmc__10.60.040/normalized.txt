(a) The purpose of this article is to provide for the issuance of annual delivery vehicles permits to businesses that deliver necessary items and materials to, and from, businesses in the central business district (CBD).
(b) The Superintendent of Transportation Operations or designee shall:
(1) Accept applications for annual delivery vehicle permits on forms provided.
(2) Approve applications upon verification of the intended vehicle use.
(3) Issue no more than one annual delivery vehicle permit to each business.
(4) Collect permit fees and deposit to the parking fund. (Ord. 17795 § 1, 2-19-02. Code 1995 § 142-326.)
