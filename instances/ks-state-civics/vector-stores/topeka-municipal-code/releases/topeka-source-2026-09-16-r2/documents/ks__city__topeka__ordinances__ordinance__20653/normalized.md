## ORDINANCE NO. 20653

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez to waive certain utility system and  connection  fees as requested by the   developer for  an  affordable  housing  development  at  2800  NW   Rochester Road pursuant to Topeka Municipal Code.

AN ORDINANCE

WHEREAS,  the  State of  Kansas has added  a  scoring  bonus for  Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) applications that have a commitment of    a fee waiver from the local municipality; and

WHEREAS, points are based on unit count and the total amount of fees   waived; and

WHEREAS,  TMC  13.10.497  allows  for  a  waiver  of  the  water  system  fee  for projects  that  offer  extraordinary  benefits  to  the  City  with  Governing  Body approval necessary for a waiver of greater than 25%; and

WHEREAS, TMC 13.20.680 allows for a waiver of the sanitary sewer  connection fee  for  projects  that  offer  extraordinary  benefits  to  the  City  with  Governing    Body approval necessary for a waiver of greater than 25%; and

WHEREAS, DW Development Group,  LLC ('Developer')  has  requested  a  fee waiver for an affordable housing development located at 2800 NW Rochester Road in order  to  acquire  bonus  points  on  their  application  for  LIHTC  through  the   Kansas Housing Resources Corporation.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, that the Governing Body makes  an  affirmative  decision to  waive  a portion  of  (1)     a portion  of  the  water  system  fees  in  the  amount  of  $13,626.83  pursuant  to    TMC 13.10.497,  and  (2)  a  portion  of  the  sanitary  sewer  collection  fee  in  the  amount  of
