(a) The areas described in this section shall be reserved for exclusive use by pedestrians and any person who rides a bicycle, skates or uses roller skates, skateboards, roller blades or other similar devices:
(1) The top of the levee system known as the Oakland Unit of the Topeka flood protection project and in such areas specifically designated and marked by appropriate signage.
(2) Regional multi-use trail segments located within the City and identified in the Topeka Bikeways Master Plan and amendments thereto.
(b) No motor vehicles shall be operated in the area designated by this section except vehicles used for governmental purposes. (Ord. 20002 § 3, 6-7-16.)
