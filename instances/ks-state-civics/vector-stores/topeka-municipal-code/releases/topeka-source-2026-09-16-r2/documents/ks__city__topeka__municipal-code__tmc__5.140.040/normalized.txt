No secondhand dealer shall receive or purchase any stolen article or receive or purchase any property from any intoxicated person or any minor, or receive or purchase property owned by any minor or property which from any cause the dealer may have reason to believe or suspect cannot be lawfully or rightfully sold by the person offering such property for sale. (Ord. 18055 § 3, 8-5-03. Code 1995 § 114-39.)
