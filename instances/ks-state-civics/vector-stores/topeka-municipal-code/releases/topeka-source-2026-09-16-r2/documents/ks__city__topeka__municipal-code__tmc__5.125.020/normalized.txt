Law enforcement officers of the City shall have access during regular business hours to the place of business of any precious metal dealer conducting business in the City. Access shall be for the purpose of periodically inspecting property pledged or purchased in the transaction of the business of the precious metal dealer, and records relating to those transactions, to determine if the dealer is complying with the provisions of this chapter. (Code 1981 § 37-56. Code 1995 § 30-187.)
