## ORDINANCE NO. 20656

INTRODUCED  BY  CITY  MANAGER  DR.  ROBERT     M. PEREZ  ESTABLISHING  A  REINVESTMENT    HOUSING INCENTIVE  DISTRICT  ('RHID')  FOR  THE   JOHNSONBETTS MEADOWS DISTRICT; AND ADOPTING A   PLAN FOR  THE  DEVELOPMENT  OF  HOUSING  AND PUBLIC FACILITIES IN THE RHID.

, on September 10, 2025, the Kansas Secretary of Commerce determined that

AN ORDINANCE

WHEREAS

the findings by the Governing Body articulated in Resolution No. 9706 meet the requirements  of

K.S.A. 12-5244 and that the Governing Body may proceed with establishing the RHID; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to K.S.A. 12-5245(b), the Governing Body of the City of   Topeka

(the 'City') has heretofore adopted Resolution No. 9786, which stated that the City is considering establishing the proposed RHID and adopting a proposed development plan for the RHID, set forth

the boundaries of the proposed RHID, provided a summary of the proposed development plan, called a public hearing  concerning the  establishment of the proposed RHID, and    provided  for

notice of such public hearing, as provided in K.S.A. 12-5245; and

WHEREAS

,  the Governing Body, on May 12, 2026, held a public hearing to consider public  comment  concerning  the  establishment  of  the  RHID  and  adoption  of  a  plan  for the

development of housing and public facilities; and

WHEREAS

, upon considering the information provided by the developer (Mesner-Hoppe

JV Development, LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company), City staff and comments from the public, the Governing Body deems it advisable to make certain findings to establish the proposed

RHID and to adopt the proposed plan.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE

CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, THAT:

Section  1. Establishment of the Johnson-Betts Meadows RHID

.     The Johnson-Betts

Meadows RHID will be established within the boundaries of the real estate legally described and attached  in

Exhibits A

and

B.

The boundaries  of the RHID do  not  contain any     property  not referenced in Resolution No. 9786, which provided notice of the public hearing.

Section 2. Approval of Development Plan ('Plan').

The Plan for the development of housing and public facilities presented on April 7, 2026 to the Governing Body, which    includes

(a) the 'Application for Creation of a RHID Housing Development Plan' received by the City on

November 18, 2025, and (b) a 'City of Topeka, Kansas Mesner-Hoppe JV Development, LLC

(Johnson  Betts  Meadows)  RHID  Project  Plan  for  an  Affordable  Rental  Housing  Project-

Financial Analysis,' dated March 20, 2026, contains the requirements specified in K.S.A. 12-

5245(a) and is hereby approved.

Section 3. Adverse Effect on Other Governmental Units.

If, within 30 days   following the conclusion of the public hearing, any of the following actions occur, this ordinance shall be

null and void.

(a)

The Board of Education for U.S.D. 501 determines, by resolution, the proposed

RHID will have an adverse effect on the school district.

(b)

The Board of Commissioners for Shawnee County determines, by resolution,   the proposed RHID will have an adverse effect on the county.

Section 4. Transmission of Documents.

The City Clerk is directed to transmit a copy  of the description of the land within the RHID, a copy of this ordinance adopting the Plan and a map

or plat indicating the boundaries of the RHID to: (a) the Shawnee County Clerk; (b) the Shawnee
