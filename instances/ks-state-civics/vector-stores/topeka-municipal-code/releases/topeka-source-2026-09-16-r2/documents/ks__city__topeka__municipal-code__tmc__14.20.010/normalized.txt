The 2024 International Building Code, first printing December 2024, as amended by TMC 14.20.060 , is hereby adopted by reference and incorporated herein as if fully set out in this chapter as the building code of the City. Additionally, the provisions contained in Appendix C, Group U – Agricultural Buildings, Appendix E, Supplementary Accessibility Requirements, Appendix O, Performance-Based Application and Appendix P, Sleeping Lofts, are specifically adopted and incorporated herein and made a part of this code. (Ord. 20648 § 1, 4-14-26.)
State Law References: Adoption by reference authorized, K.S.A. 12-3009 et seq., 12-3301 et seq.
