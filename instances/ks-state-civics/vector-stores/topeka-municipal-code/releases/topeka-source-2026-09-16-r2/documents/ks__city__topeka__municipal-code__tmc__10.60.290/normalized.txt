The Transportation Operations Superintendent and City Engineer may designate parking spaces on public streets or property throughout the City to be reserved only for use by disabled persons, and the Transportation Operations Superintendent shall mark and post such parking spaces as specified by this article. (Ord. 19370 § 95, 3-23-10. Code 1995 § 142-403.)
