(a) An off-premises retailer shall sell cereal malt beverages and beer:
(1) Only at the premises identified on the license application.
(2) Only at retail.
(3) Only in original and unopened containers.
(b) An off-premises retailer shall not:
(1) Sell cereal malt beverages and beer for consumption at the licensed premises.
(2) Permit cereal malt beverages or beer to be consumed at the licensed premises.
(3) Sell cereal malt beverages or beer through curb service or drive-up service.
(c) An on-premises retailer shall sell cereal malt beverages and beer:
(1) Only at the premises identified on the license application.
(2) Only at retail.
(d) An on-premises retailer shall not sell cereal malt beverages or beer through curb service or drive-up service. (Ord. 20175 § 24, 3-12-19.)
