The notice of violation shall include the following:
(a) Identification of the mobile home park.
(b) A statement that identifies the tree(s) in question and location in the park.
(c) A statement that the owner must remove the tree or prune the tree by the date designated in the notice.
(d) A statement advising that the owner may request an appeal hearing before an administrative hearing officer. The request shall be submitted to the Code Official on or before the date designated in the notice. The scope of the appeal shall be limited to (1) whether the provisions of this chapter apply; (2) whether the Code Official has correctly interpreted this chapter; and/or (3) whether the requirements of this chapter can be adequately satisfied by other means.
(e) A statement that if the violation(s) is not corrected or a hearing requested, the City may impose administrative penalties, abate the violation and assess the costs against the owner.
(f) A statement advising that failure to timely comply with the notice may result in prosecution in municipal court regardless whether an administrative hearing is pending. (Ord. 20502 § 4, 7-30-24.)
