Any permittee under this chapter when moving any building or structure over or along any street, avenue, alley or public grounds within the City shall comply with the provisions of this chapter. Failure by the permittee to comply with the provisions of this chapter shall result in revocation of the permit. Any permittee found guilty of violating any of the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and punished as provided in TMC 1.10.070 . (Ord. 18830 § 1, 3-13-07; Ord. 16513 § 2(10-526), 9-15-92. Code 1995 § 26-582.)
