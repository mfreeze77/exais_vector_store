The Chief of the Fire Department shall have full power and command over all persons whatever at fires and shall direct and adopt any and all measures that the Chief shall deem most advisable for the extinguishment of such fires and for the care and protection of the property endangered thereby. The Fire Chief shall have power, if need be, to summon any and all persons present to aid in extinguishing any fire, or in removing or caring for personal property from any building on fire or in danger thereof. (Ord. 20206 § 10, 9-10-19. Code 1981 § 16-23. Code 1995 § 70-34. Formerly 2.115.010.)
