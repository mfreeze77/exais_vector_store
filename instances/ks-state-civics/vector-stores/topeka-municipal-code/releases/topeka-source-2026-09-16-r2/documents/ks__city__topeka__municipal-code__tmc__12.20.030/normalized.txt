When the Traffic Engineer determines upon investigation that a traffic hazard as defined in TMC 12.20.020 exists, the Traffic Engineer shall provide notice to the property owner. Said notice and service thereof shall meet the requirements set forth in K.S.A. 8-2011 et seq. and any amendments thereto. (Ord. 19407 § 3, 4-13-10. Code 1995 § 130-553.)
