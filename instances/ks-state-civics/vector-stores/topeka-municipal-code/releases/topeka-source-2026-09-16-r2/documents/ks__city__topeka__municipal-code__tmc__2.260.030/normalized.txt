(a) The Board shall consist of seven members who reside within the boundaries of the City or Shawnee County. One member shall be a member of a neighborhood improvement association established pursuant to TMC 2.25.050 ; one member shall have professional experience in the real estate industry; one member shall have professional experience in the construction industry; one member shall be an employee of the City designated by the City Manager; one member shall be a City Council member; and two members shall be from the public at large.
(b) Board members shall be appointed by the Mayor and approved by the City Council.
(c) Terms of office shall be as follows: (1) August 1, 2023, to December 31, 2024; (2) January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025; (3) January 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
Beginning in 2027, Board members will serve staggered two-year terms. The Mayor shall appoint four Board members to serve from January 1, 2027, to December 31, 2028. These four positions shall be appointed to a two-year term commencing January 1, 2029. Three Board members shall be appointed to serve from January 1, 2027, to December 31, 2027. These three positions shall be appointed to serve two-year terms commencing January 1, 2028.
Upon expiration of a term the position shall remain vacant until a successor is appointed. Vacancies shall be filled for an unexpired term in the same manner as the original appointments were made. (Ord. 20657 § 2, 5-19-26.)
