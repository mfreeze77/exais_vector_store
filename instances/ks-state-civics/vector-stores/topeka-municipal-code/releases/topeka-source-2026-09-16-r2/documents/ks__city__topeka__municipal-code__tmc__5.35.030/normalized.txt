No person shall operate vehicles under this chapter for the carrying of persons for hire in the City on or along or within one block of the nearest property line of any street in the City on which a transportation company is operating pursuant to the terms of a franchise duly granted by the Council. (Code 1981 § 46-90. Code 1995 § 154-153.)
