Only upon written approval of the Governing Body of the City of Topeka may the rights and obligations of AMR, pursuant to this franchise ordinance, be transferred to a person meeting the requirements for an ambulance service, as determined by the laws and regulations of the State and the City at the time of the contemplated transfer. Any approved transferee shall, without limitation, assume all the duties and obligations of AMR and AMR shall be released of all future rights, duties, and obligations arising from this franchise ordinance.
