It shall be unlawful for any person, with no requirement of a culpable mental state, to rear or keep any swine in the City. This prohibition against swine shall not apply to miniature pot-belly pigs kept as pets, which are considered domestic animals pursuant to TMC 6.05.010(j) ; provided, however, that each pig meets the requirements set out in TMC 6.15.200 . (Ord. 20106 § 83, 12-19-17.)
