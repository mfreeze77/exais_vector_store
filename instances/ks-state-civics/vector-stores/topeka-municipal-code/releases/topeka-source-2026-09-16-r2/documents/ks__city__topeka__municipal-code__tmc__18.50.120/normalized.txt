It shall be the duty of the Planning Director or designee to enforce these regulations. The Planning Director or designee may require site plans and other building plans as necessary to determine compliance with these regulations prior to the issuance of a building permit or the use of property subject to these regulations. Appeal from the decision of the Planning Director or designee may be made to the Board of Zoning Appeals. (Ord. 20062 § 5, 4-18-17.)
