(a) The violation of any provision of the traffic ordinances prohibiting or restricting vehicular standing or parking, or establishing a parking violation, shall be a civil offense punishable by fine, and no criminal penalty, or civil sanction other than that prescribed herein, shall be imposed.
(b) The fines imposed for such violations are prescribed in TMC 10.60.010 . (Ord. 17636 § 2, 1-9-01. Code 1995 § 142-517.)
