If a person is convicted of a violation of any section in this title, or of any applicable State or Federal law, and the animal was transported by the Police Department or Animal Control office to an animal shelter, then the judge of the Municipal Court shall impose and collect $30.00 for handling and transportation of the animal. (Ord. 20106 § 6, 12-19-17.)
