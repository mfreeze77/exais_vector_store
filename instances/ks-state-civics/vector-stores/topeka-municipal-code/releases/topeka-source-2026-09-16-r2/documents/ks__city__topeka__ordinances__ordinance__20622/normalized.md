## AN ORDINANCE

## ORDINANCE NO. 20622

introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez annexing land to the City of Topeka, Kansas, in accordance with K.S.A. 12-520, a 25.28 acre tract located on the north side of SW 45 th  Street and approximately 1,020 feet west of SW Burlingame Road, within unincorporated Shawnee County, Kansas and adjacent to  City  of  Topeka  corporate  limits,  and  said  land  being  annexed  for  all  City purposes. (A25/03) (Council District No. 7)

## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

Section 1. That the following described land, meeting the conditions for annexation prescribed in K.S.A. 12-520(a)(7), is hereby annexed and made a part of the City of Topeka, Kansas:

A tract in the Southeast Quarter of Section 23, Township 12 South, Range 15 East of the 6th P.M., Shawnee County, Kansas, being the tract described on a Quit Claim Deed recorded as document # 2024R12026, being more particularly described by Patrick Leo LaFontaine, PS 1690, on October 27, 2025, as follows:

BEGINNING at the Southwest corner of said Southeast Quarter;

Thence N01°27'35'W (State Plane Bearings) along the Western boundary of said Southeast

Quarter a distance of 1,500.00 feet;

Thence N88°27'56'E a distance of 372.76 feet (375.00 feet described); Thence

S21°15'32'E a distance of 620.22 feet (620.00 feet described);

Thence S45°41'30'E a distance of 873.47 feet (873.61 feet described) to a point on the Westerly line of a 30foot Williams' Telecommunications Pipeline Easement per document # 2024R12026;

Thence S31°00'00'W along said Westerly line a distance of 329.85 feet (329.81 described) to a point on the Southern boundary of said Southeast Quarter;

Thence S87°49'15'W along said Southern boundary a distance of 1,015.19 feet (1,015.07 feet described) to the POINT OF BEGINNING.

Containing 26.35 acres, more or less.

Section 2. That all land described in Section 1 of this Ordinance is taken into and made a part of the City for all City purposes and is assigned to City Council District No. 7.

Section 3. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after its   passage, approval and publication in the official city newspaper.

Section 4. Upon passage and publication, the City Clerk shall file a certified copy   of this Ordinance with the County Clerk, the Register of Deeds, and the Shawnee County Election Commissioner.

## PASSED AND APPROVED by the Governing Body of the City of Topeka, Kansas on

December 16, 2025.

Michael A. Padilla, Mayor

ATTEST:

Brenda Younger, City Clerk
