## (Published in the Topeka Metro News May 19,2025)

## ORDINANCE NO. 20560

AN ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Dr. Robert M. Perez  to waive     certain building permits and other fees  as requested by the developer for economic purposes pursuant to City Code.

WHEREAS,   the  State   of   Kansas  has   added   a   scoring  bonus  for  LIHTC applications that have a commitment of a fee waiver from the local municipality;  and

WHEREAS, points are based on unit count and the total amount of fees   waived; and

WHEREAS, Section 109.6 of the International Building Code, adopted by the City and  codified  at  TMC  14.20.060,  allows  modification  of  fees  exceeding  25%  of the scheduled permits fees with approval of the Governing Body; and

WHEREAS,  TMC  13.10.497  allows  for  a  waiver  of  the  water  system  fee  for projects  that  offer  extraordinary  benefits  to  the  City  with  Governing  Body approval necessary for a waiver of greater than 25%; and

WHEREAS, TMC 13.20.680 allows for a waiver of the sanitary sewer  connection fee  for  projects  that  offer  extraordinary  benefits  to  the  City  with  Governing    Body approval necessary for a waiver of greater than 25%; and

WHEREAS, AMD Partners LLC ('Developer') has requested a fee waiver for   an affordable housing  development  on  SW  Arvonia  in order  to acquire  bonus points on their application for LIHTC through KHRC.

BE  IT  ORDAINED BY THE  COUNCIL  OF THE  CITY OF  TOPEKA, KANSAS, that the Governing Body makes an affirmative decision to waive a total of $40,000 of (a) certain building permits and plan review fees pursuant to TMC 14.20.060, (2) the   water
