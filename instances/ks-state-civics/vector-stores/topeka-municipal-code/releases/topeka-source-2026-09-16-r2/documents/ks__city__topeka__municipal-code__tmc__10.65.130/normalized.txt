(a) The City Traffic Engineer may establish taxistands or change or revise established taxistands from time to time as the public good requires.
(b) This section shall in no way affect taxistands established by the Council adjacent to hotels or the bus station in the City. (Code 1981 § 46-53. Code 1995 § 154-73.)
