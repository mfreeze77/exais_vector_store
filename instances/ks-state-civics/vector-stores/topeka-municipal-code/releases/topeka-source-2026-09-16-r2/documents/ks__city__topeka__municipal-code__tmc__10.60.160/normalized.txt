(a) The Transportation Operations Superintendent shall install parking meters or appropriate signage in or adjacent to the time-restricted parking zones established as provided in this article. Where payment is required, the payment method shall be capable of displaying the amount of time that has been purchased.
(b) Payment methods must be capable of displaying an appropriate signal that the lawful parking time period has expired, and during such period of time and prior to the expiration thereof, will indicate the interval of time that remains for such period.
(c) Payment methods must indicate the days and hours when payment is required, the value of the time purchased and the limited period of time for which parking is lawfully permitted in the time-restricted parking zone in which such space is located. (Ord. 20566 § 6, 6-10-25.)
