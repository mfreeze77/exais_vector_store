The Development Services Director shall have the right, during reasonable hours, to enter any building for the purpose of making any inspection required by this chapter, and for that purpose shall be given prompt access to all buildings, public or private, on application to the company or individual owning or in charge or control of such building. (Ord. 19072 § 9, 4-15-08. Code 1981 § 10-168. Code 1995 § 26-368.)
