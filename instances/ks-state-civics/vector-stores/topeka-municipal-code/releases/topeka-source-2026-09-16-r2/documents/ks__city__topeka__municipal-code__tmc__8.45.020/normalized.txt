It shall be unlawful for any person to start a fire or allow any person to start a fire to any pile of refuse, papers, shavings, weeds, brush, grass, leaves or other combustible material, or to make any fire of any description in the open air in the corporate limits of the City, except as provided in TMC 8.45.060 . (Code 1981 § 16-117. Code 1995 § 70-162.)
