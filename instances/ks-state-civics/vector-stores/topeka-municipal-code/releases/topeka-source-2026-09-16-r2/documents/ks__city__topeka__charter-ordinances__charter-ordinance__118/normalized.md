## CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 118

- A CHARTER ORDINANCE introduced by City Manager Brent Trout, amending City of Topeka Charter Ordinance No. 115, Section 1, as codified at Section A9-2 of the Topeka Municipal Code.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS, THAT:

Section 1. That section A9-2, Levy of tax, of the Code of the City of Topeka, Kansas, is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Levy of tax.

The governing  body  of  the  City  of  Topeka,  Kansas  mayshall  levy  a  tax  not  to exceed of four  and  two-tenths  (4.2)  mills.  The  mill  levy  shall  be  upon  all  the  taxable property within such city, as authorized by ordinance of the city, the proceeds of such tax levy to be used by the metropolitan transit authority of Topeka, Kansas, pursuant to

- Article IX of this code. 18
