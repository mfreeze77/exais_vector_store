There is hereby created and established a body which shall be known as the new Topeka Planning Commission, in place of and instead of the body heretofore known as the Topeka Planning Commission. The new Topeka Planning Commission shall assume all powers, duties, responsibilities and functions provided by the laws of the State and resolutions and ordinances of the City. Such body shall be referred to as the Topeka Planning Commission. (Ord. 20206 § 81, 9-10-19; Ord. 18442 § 2, 5-24-05; Ord. 18094 § 1, 9-23-03. Code 1981 § 33-17. Code 1995 § 110-26. Formerly 2.65.010.)
