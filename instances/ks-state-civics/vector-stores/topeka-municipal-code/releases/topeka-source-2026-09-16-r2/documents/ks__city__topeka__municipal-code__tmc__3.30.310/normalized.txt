(a) The real property transactions listed below shall require approval by the Governing Body:
(1) Acquisition of fee simple title.
(2) Acceptance of property donated by a third party.
(3) Sale of property owned by the City.
(4) Transfers to a governmental agency.
(5) Donations of property owned by the City.
(6) Leases of property that meet the conditions in TMC 3.30.350 .
(b) The City Manager is authorized to execute all documents associated with such transactions, including execution of deeds. (Ord. 20236 § 1, 1-7-20.)
