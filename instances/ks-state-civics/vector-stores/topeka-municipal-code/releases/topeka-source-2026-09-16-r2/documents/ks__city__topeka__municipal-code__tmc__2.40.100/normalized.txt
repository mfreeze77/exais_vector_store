The Governing Body of the City shall provide at the expense of the City a suitable courtroom for the Municipal Court, together with all necessary supplies and records. Municipal Court shall be held at such time and places designated by the Municipal Judge. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 9, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-44. Formerly 2.110.100.)
