Any person who violates any of the provisions of TMC 9.50.010 through 9.50.070 within the corporate limits of the City shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be punished by a fine not to exceed $2,500, or by imprisonment not to exceed one year, or both such fine and imprisonment. (Ord. 18136 § 9, 12-2-03. Code 1995 § 54-207.)
