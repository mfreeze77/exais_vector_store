If, upon inspection of a mobile home park, a violation is found and cited by the Development Services Division, an additional per hour fee shall be charged to the mobile home licensee in the amount as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule for time spent investigating such violation. Imposition of the per hour fee shall be limited to the following:
(a) Electrical service size as owned by the mobile home park;
(b) Streets;
(c) Sidewalks;
(d) Lighting;
(e) Gas line installations as owned by the mobile home park;
(f) Septic tanks;
(g) Sewerage system as owned by the mobile home park; and
(h) Water line system as owned by the mobile home park. (Ord. 20631 § 23, 1-13-26.)
