Appeals of a violation of the City code or the imposition of a fee, charge or penalty shall be governed by this chapter unless a different appeal procedure is specified under this Code or by State or Federal law. (Ord. 20206 § 13, 9-10-19; Ord. 19319 § 1, 10-13-09. Code 1995 § 2-486. Formerly 2.145.010.)
