The records custodian may prescribe reasonable fees for providing access to or furnishing copies of public records. Pursuant to KSA 45-219 , fees shall not exceed the actual cost of furnishing copies of public records, including the cost of staff time required to make the information available. (Ord. 20631 § 2, 1-13-26. Formerly 2.20.120.)
State Law References: Fees for records, K.S.A. 45-218 , 45-219 .
