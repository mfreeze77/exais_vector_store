Miniature pot-belly pigs shall be confined inside the owner’s dwelling/residence; or in a shelter with its sides embedded at least two feet into the ground or an underlayment that will prevent the animal from digging out of the pen and/or running at large. The animal shall be provided with sufficient bedding during the winter season and a water source of sufficient size and quantity that will allow them to cool themselves during the summer season. There shall be compliance with all requirements set out in TMC 6.05.100(a)(3)(iii) . (Ord. 20106 § 38, 12-19-17.)
