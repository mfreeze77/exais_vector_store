(a) No significant industrial user shall discharge wastewater into the POTW without first obtaining an individual wastewater discharge permit.
(b) The Utilities Director may require other users to obtain individual wastewater discharge permits as necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
(c) Any violation of the terms and conditions of an individual wastewater discharge permit shall be deemed a violation of this chapter and subject the wastewater discharge permittee to the enforcement actions, including administrative monetary penalties, set out in this chapter. Obtaining an individual wastewater discharge permit does not relieve a permittee of its obligation to comply with all Federal and State pretreatment standards or requirements or with any other requirements of Federal, State and local law. (Ord. 20089 § 62, 10-17-17.)
