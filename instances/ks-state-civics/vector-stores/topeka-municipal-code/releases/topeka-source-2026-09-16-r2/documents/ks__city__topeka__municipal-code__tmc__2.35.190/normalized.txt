Any complaint received alleging that a no-knock warrant was served shall be presented to the Chief of Police who shall refer the matter to the Professional Standards Unit for investigation and such investigation shall be subject to review by the Independent Police Auditor. The results of the investigation shall be provided to the complainant, in writing, within seven days following the completion of the investigation, which shall occur no later than one year after receipt of the complaint or in accordance with time frames specified in any applicable collective bargaining agreement, whichever is shorter. (Ord. 20249 § 4, 7-21-20.)
