Any liquid waste hauler permit may be revoked by the Utilities Director upon the occurrence of any of the following:
(a) Failure to pay disposal charges for a period of 60 days.
(b) Failure to electronically provide or maintain manifest records.
(c) Submission of inaccurate or false information concerning waste characterization.
(d) Attempted disposal or disposal of hazardous waste.
(e) Disposal of waste into the City’s system at any site other than the designated site. (Ord. 20157 § 7, 12-4-18.)
