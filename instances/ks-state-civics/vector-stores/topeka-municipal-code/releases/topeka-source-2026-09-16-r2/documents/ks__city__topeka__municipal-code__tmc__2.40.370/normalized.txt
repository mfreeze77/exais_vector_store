Accused persons shall be arraigned:
(a) At the time specified in the notice to appear, or in the appearance bond; or
(b) If no date be specified, then on the earliest date when the court convenes. (Ord. 20206 § 9, 9-10-19; Ord. 17666 § 39, 3-27-01. Code 1995 § 50-74. Formerly 2.110.370.)
