(e) Late registration or renewal fee shall be assessed as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule.
(f) Individuals may elect to split their biennial renewal fees pursuant to subsections (a) and (b) of this section into two equal payments with one-half of the fee due on the last working day before December 15th of the first year and the remaining one-half of the fee due on the last working day before December 15th of the second year. An administrative fee as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule shall be paid in addition to the renewal fee and accompany the first one-half renewal payment. Failure to pay the amount due under this subsection on the date that it is due shall result in late fees being assessed pursuant to subsection (e) of this section. (Ord. 20631 § 33, 1-13-26.)
