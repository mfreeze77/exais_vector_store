The payment of a trafficway impact fee under this chapter does not relieve a developer or other property owner from paying:
(a) Taxes and special assessments;
(b) Fees for permits or inspections;
(c) Fees for connections to utilities;
(d) Taxes, assessments or fees imposed, levied or collected by other governmental agencies including subdivisions of the State and Federal government. (Ord. 18273 § 5, 6-22-04. Code 1995 § 130-764.)
