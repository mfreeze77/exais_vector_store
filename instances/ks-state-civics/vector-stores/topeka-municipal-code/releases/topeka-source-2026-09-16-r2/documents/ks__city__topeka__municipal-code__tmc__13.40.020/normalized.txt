(a) The City of Topeka property owner’s guide to stormwater BMP maintenance referenced in this chapter shall be considered an integral part of this chapter without separate adoption. Where provisions of this chapter conflict with the handbook, this chapter shall control. Permissive and advisory provisions in the handbook shall not be construed as mandatory.
(b) Within the referenced handbook, the Director may allow administrative variations to requirements and guidance stated provided such variations do not result in a reduction or elimination of stormwater performance criteria for any stormwater management facility. (Ord. 20262 § 11, 10-6-20.)
