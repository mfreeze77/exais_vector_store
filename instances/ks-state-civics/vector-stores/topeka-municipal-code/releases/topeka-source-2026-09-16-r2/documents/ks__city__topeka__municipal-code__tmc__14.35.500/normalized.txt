The purpose of this article is to:
(a) Protect the public potable water supply of the City from pollution or contamination due to cross connection in accordance with the provisions of K.S.A. 65-163a , 65-171g , and any administrative regulations thereto;
(b) Prohibit and eliminate all cross connections within the public potable water supply system; and
(c) Provide for the maintenance of a continuing effective cross connection control program and thus protect the public health. (Code 1981 § 10-292. Code 1995 § 26-447.)
