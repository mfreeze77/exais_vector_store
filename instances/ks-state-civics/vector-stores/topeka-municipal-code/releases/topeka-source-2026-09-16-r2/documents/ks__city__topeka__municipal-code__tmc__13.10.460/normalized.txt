(a) Any person who damages or impairs the operation of a fire hydrant shall immediately notify the Water Division.
(b) Each owner of a private fire hydrant shall notify both the Fire Chief and the Utilities Director if the owner has knowledge that a private water hydrant has been damaged or is inoperable. (Ord. 20089 § 23, 10-17-17.)
