The Traffic Engineer and the Transportation Operations Superintendent are hereby authorized to conduct surveys and studies to determine the necessity for and suitable locations where parking spaces may be set aside on public streets or property throughout the City and designated as being reserved only for use by disabled persons with a State-issued license plate or placard. Angular parking spaces so designated shall be a minimum width of 12 feet, and easy access to and from all spaces shall be provided for disabled persons. (Ord. 19370 § 94, 3-23-10. Code 1995 § 142-402.)
