No taxicab business license shall be issued until the person applying therefor shall file with the City Clerk an application stating:
(a) The number of cars to be operated by the applicant, which in no instance shall be less than eight in number;
(b) The location of its central office in the City, which shall have a telephone therein installed, operated and maintained on a 24-hour-a-day service. (Code 1981 § 46-64. Code 1995 § 154-87.)
