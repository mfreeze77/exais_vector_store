It shall be unlawful for any person to occupy, lodge or sleep in any vacant or unoccupied barn, garage, shed, shop or other building or structure, or any automobile, truck, railroad car or other vehicle without owning the same, or without permission of the owner or person entitled to possession of the same, or sleep in any vacant lot during the hours of darkness. (Code 1981 § 15-72. Code 1995 § 54-130.)
