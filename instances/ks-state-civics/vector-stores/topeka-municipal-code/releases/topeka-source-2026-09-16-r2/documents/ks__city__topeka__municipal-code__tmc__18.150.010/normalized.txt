This district is established to provide for those commercial activities which serve a major segment of the total community population. In addition to a variety of retail goods and services, these centers may typically feature a number of large traffic generators that require access from major thoroughfares. The extent and range of activities permitted are in the moderate to medium intensity range with a prohibition on outside display and storage of supplies, materials, products, and equipment, except for display of gardening and yard supplies and permitted vehicles for sale. (Ord. 19921 § 94, 9-23-14.)
