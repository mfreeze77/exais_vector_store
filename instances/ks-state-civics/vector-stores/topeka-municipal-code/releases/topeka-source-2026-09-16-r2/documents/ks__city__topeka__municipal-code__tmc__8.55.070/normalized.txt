(a) Any alarm system that has been designated a nuisance system may be required by the Fire Chief or designee to undergo a service inspection and certification by a fire alarm contractor with a current license in accordance with Chapter 5.15 TMC.
(b) Failure to provide a certification required by subsection (a) of this section may result in the Fire Chief or designee ordering a fire watch in accordance with adopted codes. (Ord. 20121 § 7, 5-15-18.)
