Most-Favored Municipality. Should MCImetro after the Parties' execution and delivery of this Agreement enter into a franchise agreement with another municipality of the same size or smaller than the City in this State, which agreement contains financial benefits for such municipality which, taken as a whole and balanced with the other terms of such agreement, are in the City's reasonable opinion substantially superior to those in this Franchise Agreement, the City shall have the right to require that MCImetro, and all similarly situated providers, modify their Franchise Agreement to incorporate the same or substantially similar superior benefits. All modifications to franchise agreements under this Section 16 shall be competitively neutral and not unreasonable or discriminatory in any way. (Ord. 20628 § 16, 1-13-26.)
