As a condition of this Contract Franchise Ordinance, Local Provider is required to obtain and is responsible for any necessary permit, license, certification, grant, registration or any other authorization required by any appropriate governmental entity, including, but not limited to, the City, the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) or the Kansas Corporation Commission (KCC), subject to Local Provider’s right to challenge in good faith such requirements as established by the FCC, KCC or other City Ordinance. Local Provider shall also comply with all applicable laws, statutes and/or ordinances, subject to Local Provider’s right to challenge in good faith such laws, statutes and/or ordinances. (Ord. 19468 § 7, 9-21-10.)
