(a) All applications for an off-premises retailer license shall be accompanied by a license fee as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule, which fee shall be in addition to any fee charged by the State of Kansas.
(b) All applications for an on-premises retailer license shall be accompanied by a license fee as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule, which fee shall be in addition to any fee charged by the State of Kansas.
(c) In addition to the foregoing license fee each applicant shall pay administrative fees for inspection or reinspection of the premises as prescribed by the City’s fee schedule. (Ord. 20631 § 6, 1-13-26.)
