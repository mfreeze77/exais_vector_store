(a) During the term of the franchise grant and during such time as the grantee is providing service pursuant to such grant, there shall be on file with the City Clerk an insurance policy, approved as to form and endorsed by the City Attorney, providing liability coverage for each and every ambulance owned, operated or leased by the grantee.
(b) Minimum coverage of the insurance policy required by this section shall be in the amounts of $1,000,000 for any one person killed or injured in any one accident or occurrence and $2,000,000 for more than one person injured or killed in any one accident or occurrence, with passenger or patient hazard included in the policy. Such policy shall also provide a minimum coverage of $100,000 for all damages arising out of injury to or destruction of property. Each insurance policy shall include medical professional liability insurance in a minimum amount of $1,000,000. (Ord. 20557 § 5, 4-8-25.)
