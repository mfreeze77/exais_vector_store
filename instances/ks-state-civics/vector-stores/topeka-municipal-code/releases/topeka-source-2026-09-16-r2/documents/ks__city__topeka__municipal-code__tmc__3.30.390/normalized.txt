In addition to the definitions found in TMC 2.25.010 , the following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning:
“Bid” means the amount of labor and material required to do a specific contract work by a contractor.
“Contractor” means the party who is to do the work if the bid is approved that the contractor submits. (Ord. 18263 § 15, 6-8-04. Code 1981 § 13-135. Code 1995 § 42-62.)
