It shall be unlawful for any person, with no requirement of a culpable mental state, to allow or permit domestic fowl or poultry to run or be at large at any time in the City. Fowl must be kept on the owner’s property, within a fenced area and inside a fully enclosed pen. The fact of any domestic fowl being found at large shall be prima facie evidence of a violation of this section. (Ord. 20106 § 64, 12-19-17.)
