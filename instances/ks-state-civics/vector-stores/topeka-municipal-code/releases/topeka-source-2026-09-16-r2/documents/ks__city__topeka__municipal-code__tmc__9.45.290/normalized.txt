The erection (including excavating), demolition, exterior alteration or exterior repair of any building or exterior construction project is prohibited between the hours of 7:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. Exceptions may be granted in the interest of public health and safety with authorization from the City Manager or designee. (Ord. 19775 § 4, 10-23-12.)
