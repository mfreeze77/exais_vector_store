The decision of the Governing Body to deny a license may be appealed to the district court pursuant to K.S.A. 60-2101 and amendments thereto. Any appeal taken from an order suspending or revoking any such license shall not suspend the order of suspension or revocation during the pendency of any such appeal. In the case of the revocation of the license of any licensee, no new license shall be issued to such person or any person acting for or on his behalf for a period of six months thereafter. (Ord. 20175 § 17, 3-12-19.)
