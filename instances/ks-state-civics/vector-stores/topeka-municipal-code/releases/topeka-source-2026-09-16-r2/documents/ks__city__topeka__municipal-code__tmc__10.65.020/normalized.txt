The provisions of this chapter and Chapter 5.155 TMC shall not apply to any vehicle hired by parents or guardians of school age children exclusively for the transportation of their school age children to and from school, home and school-related activities, any funeral coach, limousine or other passenger vehicle for hire while being used for services at funerals, weddings or other celebrations. (Ord. 16896 § 1, 12-19-95. Code 1981 § 46-51. Code 1995 § 154-62.)
