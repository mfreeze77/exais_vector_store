Any passenger aggrieved by any act or conduct of a taxicab driver may cause a complaint to be filed in writing with the City Attorney, who shall investigate the complaint, and who may make recommendations for suspension or revocation of the taxicab driver’s license to the Council. (Code 1981 § 46-85. Code 1995 § 154-72.)
