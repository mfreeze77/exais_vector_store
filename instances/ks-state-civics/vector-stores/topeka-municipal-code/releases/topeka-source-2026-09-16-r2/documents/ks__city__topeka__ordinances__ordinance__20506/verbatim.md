## ORDINANCE NO. 20506

AN  ORDINANCE  INTRODUCED  BY  CITY  MANAGER  ROBERT  PEREZ AUTHORIZING CERTAIN WASTEWATER IMPROVEMENTS TO THE CITY OF  TOPEKA,  KANSAS'S  COMBINED  UTILITY  SYSTEM;  AMENDING  AND SUPPLEMENTING ORDINANCE NO. 20487 OF THE CITY AND SUBSTITUTING SUCH WASTEWATER IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE PROJECT PREVIOUSLY IDENTIFIED IN SUCH ORDINANCE; AND AUTHORIZING  THE  USE  OF  THE  LOAN  OBLIGATION  AND  BONDS AUTHORIZED THEREUNDER TO FINANCE SUCH WASTEWATER IMPROVEMENTS.

WHEREAS, the City of Topeka, Kansas (the 'Municipality' and the 'City') is a municipality as said  term  is  defined  in  K.S.A.  65-3321  through  65-3329,  inclusive  (the  'Loan  Act'),  which  operates  a wastewater collection and treatment system (the 'Wastewater System'); and

WHEREAS , the Municipality is authorized under the provisions of the K.S.A. 10-1201 et seq. (the 'Bond Act'),  to  issue  and  sell  revenue  bonds  for  the  purpose  of  paying  all  or  part  of  the  cost  of  the acquisition, construction, reconstruction, alteration, repair, improvement, extension or enlargement of the Municipality's water, water pollution control and stormwater utilities, including the Wastewater System (collectively, the 'System'), provided that the principal of and interest on such revenue bonds shall be payable solely from the Net Revenues derived by the Municipality from the operation of the System; and

WHEREAS , the Municipality has, pursuant to the Loan Act and Bond Act, passed Ordinance No. 20487,  which  authorized  and  approved  a  loan  to  the  City  from  the  Kansas  Water  Pollution  Control Revolving Fund (the 'Revolving Fund'), and the issuance of the Municipality's Combined Utility Revenue Bonds,  Series  2024-A  (the  'Series 2024-A  Bonds'),  in  order  to  finance  certain  Wastewater  System improvements; and

WHEREAS ,  the  governing  body  of  the hereby  finds  and  determines  that  it  is necessary  and  advisable  to  amend  and  supplement  Ordinance  No.  20487  so  that  the  'Project'  defined therein shall now mean and include construction of a project to improve and rehabilitate the Municipality's Shunga  Pump  Station  (291058.00),  Shunga  Force  Main  (291110.00),  Grant-Jefferson  pump  station (291061.00) and Grant-Jefferson Force Main (291144.00) (collectively, the 'Project'); and

WHEREAS ,  Ordinance No. 20487 defines the terms 'Obligation' and 'Agreement,' and such terms and all other terms capitalized herein but not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning set forth in such Ordinance No. 20487; and

,

WHEREAS, the  governing  body  of  the  Municipality  hereby  finds  and  determines  that  it  is necessary and advisable to authorize the Project described herein and to finance the costs of such Project by the Obligation under the Agreement and by the Series 2024-A Bonds; and

WHEREAS ,  by  proceedings  duly  had,  the  governing  body  of  the  Municipality  has  previously authorized, and has declared or declares its intention under the Bond Act, to undertake the Project, and to issue its Combined Utility Revenue Bonds; notices of such intention were published one time in the official newspaper of the Municipality and no sufficient written protest thereto was filed with the Clerk within fifteen (15) days after the dates of said publications, all as set forth in the Bond Act.

## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF TOPEKA, KANSAS:

- Section 1. Project  Authorization. It  is  hereby  authorized,  ordered  and  directed  that  the System be improved by constructing the Project.
- Section 2 . Project  Financing. The  total  approved  estimated  costs  of  the  Project  are $47,513,108.    A  portion  of  the  costs  of  the  Project  and  associated  financing  costs  in  the  amount  of $20,501,902 shall be payable from the proceeds of the Obligation under the Agreement and the Series 2024A Bonds.
- Section 3. Amendment of Ordinance. Ordinance No. 20487 of the Municipality is hereby amended  and  supplemented  so  that  'Project'  shall  be  defined  and  have  the  meaning  set  forth  above. Proceeds of the Obligation under the Agreement and the Series 2024-A Bonds are hereby authorized to be expended to pay costs of the Project.  Ordinance No. 20487, as amended and supplemented hereby, remains in full force and effect.
- Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect and be in full force from and after its  passage  by  the  governing  body  of  the  Municipality,  approval  by  the  Mayor,  and  publication  of  the Ordinance or a summary thereof in the official newspaper of the Municipality.
