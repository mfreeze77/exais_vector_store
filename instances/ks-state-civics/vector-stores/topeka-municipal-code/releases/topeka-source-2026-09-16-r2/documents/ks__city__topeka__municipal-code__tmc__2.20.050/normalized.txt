(a) Created – Director. There is hereby created a Department of Information Technology. The Director of Information Technology, also known as the Chief Information Officer, shall be appointed by the City Manager. The City Manager, pursuant to Charter Ordinance No. 94, Section 1 (Appendix A , Section A2-54 ), shall be responsible for supervising and evaluating the performance of the Information Technology Director, who shall be responsible for the overall operation of the Department. (Ord. 20400 § 4, 12-20-22.)
