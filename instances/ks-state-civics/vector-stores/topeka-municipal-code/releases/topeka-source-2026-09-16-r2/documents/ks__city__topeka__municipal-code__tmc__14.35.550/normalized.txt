Devices or assemblies installed, on or in a building’s potable water system, for protection against backflow or backsiphonage shall be maintained in good working condition by the owner of the devices or assemblies. All devices or assemblies shall be maintained so they will be accessible for regular inspection and testing.
The Backflow and Cross Connection Enforcement Official may inspect devices or assemblies and if found to be defective or inoperative shall require the repair or replacement thereof. (Ord. 20196 § 7, 7-23-19.)
