All wholesalers and distributors selling or delivering any cereal malt beverages within the City shall, upon demand of any law enforcement officer, turn over to the law enforcement officer samples of any cereal malt beverages in their possession for the purpose of testing the alcoholic content of such beverages. (Ord. 20175 § 21, 3-12-19.)
