It shall be unlawful for any person, with no requirement of a culpable mental state, to allow or permit any dog or miniature pot-belly pig to run or be at large, as defined in TMC 6.05.010 , at any time in the City. The fact of a dog or miniature pot-belly pig being found at large shall be prima facie evidence of a violation of this section. (Ord. 20106 § 9, 12-19-17.)
