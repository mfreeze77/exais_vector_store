It shall be unlawful for any person, with no requirement of a culpable mental state, to keep or maintain any domestic fowl or poultry upon any private premises in the City (when such keeping is lawful under other ordinances of the City) within 50 feet of any dwelling other than that of the owner or tenant of the premises on which such fowl are kept or maintained, or to keep or maintain such fowl at any time on any premises in a manner or condition constituting a public nuisance. (Ord. 20106 § 63, 12-19-17.)
