Any person desiring to move any building or structure over or along any street, avenue, alley or public grounds within the City shall, before beginning the removal of such building or structure, obtain a moving permit as provided in this article. (Ord. 16513 § 9(10-569), 9-15-92. Code 1995 § 26-606.)
State Law References: Permit required, K.S.A. 17-1914 .
